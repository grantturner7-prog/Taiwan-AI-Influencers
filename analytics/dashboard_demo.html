Decision tree (6.3), as coded
  1. Any account-standing gate open? Yes: pause. A restriction open over 14 days, or a second Ground-rule-6 event within 8 weeks, shows Retire.
  2. Enough data (8 organic posts per platform in weeks 3 to 6)? No: extend to week 8 and re-run; still no: hold, no scoring.
  3. Effort breach after one redesign (operator over 15 min per approval, N7 over 40, wife over 20 per master, rejection over 30%)? Yes: retire-review. Over the line with no redesign logged: Fix, and do the redesign first.
  4. Median ER under 0.3%? Yes: retire-review.
  5. ER 0.3% to under 0.6%, or Keep conditions partly unmet? Yes: Fix, one variable for 4 weeks, then re-test; still under after the re-test: retire-review.
  6. ER 0.6% to under 2.0%, reach not falling, effort within Keep, rejection 20% or less? Yes: Keep.
  7. ER 2.0% or more and saves + shares one third of interactions, reach rising, effort and rejection within Scale, no standing event in the window, and a lead signal or link clicks in 3 of 6 weeks? Yes: Scale-eligible, then the capacity check (6.4).
Definitions
  • Week n = days 7(n-1) to 7n-1 after the persona's first published post. Weeks 1 and 2 are warm-up.
  • Organic = not sponsored, gifted or affiliate. Sponsored posts are excluded from every median.
  • ER = (saves + shares + comments) / reach, per post, only the terms the platform reported. Where reach is blank the denominator is views, and that is labelled. Account figure = median of posts. Likes and link clicks are excluded.
  • Blank is not zero. A platform that does not report saves leaves them blank; they are never treated as 0.
  • Review-rejection rate counts any first-pass return or rejection.
Sample-size caveats
  • Each account figure is the median of at least 8 and usually 8 to 15 posts. At these sizes one or two unusual posts, one viral share or one missing metrics snapshot can move a median across a threshold. The dots show the spread; look at them.
  • Small accounts have few interactions per post, so ER moves in steps (each row shows how much one interaction is worth). A difference of one or two interactions is not a finding.
  • Posts whose latest snapshot is under 7 days old can still gain reach and interactions; the weekly report counts them.
  • Nothing here is a statistical test. The report calls a difference real only if it is at least 1.5x and both sides have at least 4 posts, and even then as a hypothesis to test.
  • Platform metric availability (reach, saves, link clicks) in Taiwan is unverified (HUMAN_TODO 10). Platforms are never compared with each other on ER.

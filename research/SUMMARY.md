## vi. Legal gotchas (nearly all statute detail unverified)
1. 薦證廣告 applies to the studio. A persona saying "I tried it" is a false statement about a non-existent experience. Whether the persona counts as an endorser is a lawyer question.
2. Paid persona ads need the platform AI-disclosure control. Meta was reportedly fined NT$5M (reports dated 2026-09-23; decision date and articles unverified) [PL51].
3. Likeness: Life8 shows a resemblance claim alone forced an apology [CP6]. Run a look-alike check on every face.
4. Purely AI-generated art may not be copyrightable (recalled, unverified), which weakens licensing.
5. Finance borders a licensing regime for investment advice (recalled). Default: no referral/affiliate links for brokers, funds, cards, loans, insurance, crypto, supplements or health devices.
6. Food claims carry fines up to NT$5,000,000 at the medical-effect tier [NI7]. A Tainan bureau warned about AI-synthesised people claiming weight loss. Cosmetics tier disputed.
7. Facebook personas must be Pages [PL34]. Plan as if Instagram's AI-generated-profile limit applies (Taiwan scope unconfirmed) [PL27].
8. Registration, tax, residency and the PDPA (DMs/chatbots sending data to foreign LLMs) are unresolved. The FTC is evaluating mandatory AI-ad disclosure [CP4].

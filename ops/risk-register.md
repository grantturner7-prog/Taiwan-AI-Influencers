## 1. How to read this register

**Scales (all *assumption*).** Likelihood is for the 8-week test and the six months after it.

| Likelihood | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L1 Low | Could happen; nothing in the project suggests it will |
| L2 Medium | Plausible; there is a precedent in the research or a known weak spot in the plan |
| L3 High | Expect it at least once |

| Impact | Meaning, in the order of the brief's success measures ([BRIEF.md](../BRIEF.md) section 1: accounts in good standing, then engagement, then revenue above cost) |
|---|---|
| I1 Minor | One post, fixed inside 24 hours |
| I2 Moderate | A persona on one platform paused or reworked for days to weeks; or a week of one person's time |
| I3 Major | A persona or platform presence lost; a legal letter; a week of both people's time |
| I4 Severe | Several presences or the studio's ability to operate lost; a money claim; an ownership dispute between the two of you |

**Score = L x I** (1 to 12). **P1** = 8 or more; **P2** = 5 to 7; **P3** = 4 or less. *Severe-tail rule:* any I4 risk is at least P2 however unlikely.

**Owner** is a person, as the brief requires: Operator, Wife, or Both with a named lead. The lawyer and the 記帳士 are consulted, never owners. **Trigger** is the observation that moves a risk from "watch" to "act"; the action itself is in the playbook scenario named in the row. Numbers in triggers come from [portfolio.md 6.2](../strategy/portfolio.md) unless marked *assumption*.

**Reading the ratings.** The ratings assume the controls described in the row are carried out. Today none of them is operating (section 4), so the true state is worse than the table.

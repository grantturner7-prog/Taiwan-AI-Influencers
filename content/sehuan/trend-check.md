# 色環 (sehuan) weekly trend-check routine

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5); graphics wording updated the same day for decision D3.** A 30-minute weekly routine that uses only public pages read by hand. Nothing here has been run; no page named below was opened in this session (web access was limited), so every statement about what a page shows or requires is *unverified* until the operator tries it. Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife; the operator reads the English gloss. Evidence labels: *assumption* = a planning figure to calibrate; *unverified* = not checked against a primary source; *computed* = calculated in this session; DEMO = format only, no real data.

## 1. What the routine is for, and what it is not for

**For:** picking next week's hook (a season, a shopping date, a search term people use), catching Taiwan phrasing that has aged or turned cringe, spotting a platform-policy change early, deciding whether the weather or the calendar means a post should be paused, and, once a month, finding **one named public source** for the 「報導放上色環」 trend explainer (series C, `series.md` section 5).

**Not for:** copying a viral format, chasing a meme, predicting what will be fashionable, or deciding what the persona "thinks". A trend never overrides the pillars (`personas/sehuan/bible.md` section 6). The persona has no lived experience, so most fashion trends are closed to it by Ground rule 4: outfit of the day, get ready with me, haul, try-on, "what I wore", fit checks, before and after, dupes (`research/culture.md` 2.3). The default outcome of a trend check is **no change to next week's masters**.

## 6. The monthly trend explainer 「報導放上色環」: protocol

Series C variant, at most once a month, first possible in January 2027 after four weeks of routine runs. Public runway shows, brand lookbooks and trend-forecast press releases are the allowed source types; the persona **reports** them and never recommends, predicts or shows them.

| Step | Who | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Pick | Operator | Choose **one** public page: a published show page, a lookbook page, or a trend-forecast press release that opens without a login | Not a paywalled report; not a social post; not a page that shows only photographs with no text |
| 2 Record | Operator | In the source slip: page title, publisher, URL, the date on the page, the date read, and the one sentence that names the colours | Never invent a source, a date or a quote. If the page cannot be opened, the post is not made |
| 3 Map | Operator | Place the colours the sentence names on the wheel as swatches drawn as SVG (the operator chooses the hex and checks the render, Claude draws it; label them 示意色，非品牌色票) | No crop, trace or colour-pick from the source's photographs; no brand logo; no product image |
| 4 Word it | Claude drafts | 「{來源名稱}（{YYYY/MM/DD}）報導指出，本季{季節}的色彩包含{色名}。」 followed by a plain "how to read it" slide and the blank wheel | Say 報導指出; never 會流行, 必備, 今年一定; no price; no brand product |
| 5 Sanity check | Fashion contact (gate G10) | Is the sentence a fair reading of the page? Is the colour naming reasonable? | A sanity check, never an approval; the outcome is logged |
| 6 Language | Wife | Taiwan wording; colour names; no mainland-leaning trend vocabulary | |
| 7 Footer | Operator | Source line with name and date: 「資料來源：{頁面名稱}｜{YYYY/MM/DD}」 | The full citation sits in the source slip; the footer naming a brand is subject to section 2 rule 10 |
| 8 Re-check | Operator | 30 days after posting, re-open the page; if it changed, post a correction (`calendar-30d.md` 7.3) | Within 24 hours |

**Allowed wording pattern (DRAFT):** 「報導指出」「公開資料顯示」「這篇整理報導裡提到的色名，並放上色環」. **Not allowed:** 「本季必買色」「這個色最好看」「照著穿就對了」「會流行」.

**What a good month looks like:** one post, one source, one sentence, one wheel; the viewer learns how to read a trend report, not what to buy.

## 8. Filters: what a trend may become

A trend is used only if it passes **all four** filters; otherwise it is logged as "not used" with the reason.

| Filter | Question | Fail example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Ground rules | Does it need a lived experience, a result claim, a body, skin, health, finance or politics angle, a hand or a second person in the picture, a brand or a price? | "OOTD", "try-on", "haul", "what I bought on 雙11", "平替" |
| 2 Pillar fit | Does it fit P1 (colour formulas), P2 (climate layers), P3 (occasions) or P4 (reading information)? | A dance sound with no colour or layering content |
| 3 Calendar and taboo | Is the day clear (section 4)? No gift taboo, no mourning colours, no number four as a count, no camp colour pair? | A post on polling day; an all-black party outfit |
| 4 Source | Can every number and every claim be sourced with a date before the post? | A "colour of the year" claim without a named page and a date |

| Trend type | Allowed action |
|---|---|
| A rising everyday search (a season word, an event outfit) | Plan a pillar post with its own logic, at the next free slot |
| A shopping date (雙12, 週年慶, 618) | A "how to read the product page" post days before, with no price or sale wording and no brand |
| A new colour word or phrase from the wife's scan | Use it only after she rules, and never as a hook with a personal claim |
| A format that needs a camera on a person, a mirror, a fitting room, a street or a home | Not used (addendum 5.4) |
| A public trend report or runway show | Only through the protocol of section 6, at most once a month |
| A controversy about AI images, labels, AI models in fashion or a brand's AI mistake | Not commented on by the persona. The operator reads it for policy risk only |
| A political, health, body-image or finance topic | Not used. If it appears in comments, hide it (`reply-guidelines.md`) |

# 色環 (sehuan) series formats: three repeatable formats

**Status: DRAFT v0.2 (v0.1 written 2026-10-12, Phase 5; v0.2 applies the 2026-10-12 decisions D6, OPS-D1 and D3 of `DECISIONS.md`: Reel templates carry the D6 conditions, the episode tables lose the two masters deferred by OPS-D1, and the diagram layer is Claude-drawn SVG).** Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife; the operator reads the English gloss. Nothing here is approved or published. The series follow the four pillars in `personas/sehuan/bible.md` section 6 and the photoreal addendum (`personas/sehuan/photoreal-addendum.md`, which wins where the two differ). The dates are the 30-day calendar's (`content/sehuan/calendar-30d.md`), where W0 = Mon 2026-11-16 is an assumption. Evidence labels: *assumption* = a planning figure to calibrate; *unverified* = not checked against a primary source; *computed* = calculated in this session.

## 1. Why series, and what "repeatable" means here

A series is a fixed **structure** with new **content** every time. The structure is what lets one operator produce four posts a week inside 6 hours (`portfolio.md` 5.1; six if the ramp after OPS-D1 ever passes) and lets a viewer recognise a post in the feed. The content (the rule, the colours, the example, the question) is always new.

Two platform points make the distinction matter (both second-hand and unverified, from `research/platforms.md`): Instagram excludes accounts that mostly repost others' pictures from recommendation, so all 色環 material is original (flats and diagrams drawn as SVG by Claude from the studio's own proportions), and the risk is not reposting but one identical-looking post every day, which the three-series mix avoids; and YouTube's inauthentic-content rule is not relevant here because 色環 has no YouTube presence in the pilot (bible C14).

The three series, each tied to the pillars:

| Series | Name | Pillar | One-line promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 轉一格 (Turn one step) | P1 配色公式 | One colour relationship, shown in three illustrations, then changed by exactly one step |
| B | 三步整理 (Three-step round-up): B1 三層順序 (three layers) and B2 三關檢查 (three checks) | P2 氣候穿搭 and P3 場合穿搭 | An order or a checklist of exactly three items that a viewer can reuse |
| C | 一詞看懂 (One word, understood) | P4 看懂標示與趨勢 | One term from a size chart, label, colour name or report, explained with a blank template |

Shared rules for all three series (from the bible and the ground rules; none is new):

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Disclosure in four places on every post: bio, first caption line (the right variant of `calendar-30d.md` 7.3), on-screen badge on every image, and the platform's AI label switched on by hand. |
| 2 | The character appears as still images from the golden set (R01 master, R02, R08b, R13, E-headshots). **Under decision D6 a still, or a slow pan or zoom of a still, may also appear inside a Reel or Short, only with all four of: the platform AI label on; the persistent badge 「AI 生成｜虛擬角色」 on every frame; no animation of the face (no blinking, lip-sync or morphing); no synthetic voice (captions or human-recorded sound only).** Face animation, synthetic voice and lip-sync stay barred until a separate video addendum and review exist (addendum risk J). Diagram motion and a pan or zoom of a still are the only motion. |
| 3 | **Presenter, not wearer (C1).** Outfits in a lesson are generic flats on a faceless dress form. She stands beside the lesson as a cut-out, never on the dress form and never in the taught outfit. Her three outfits are her fixed design and are never taught. |
| 4 | Every colour proportion is labelled 口訣（經驗法則）, never "the rule". Every number is either tagged 範例數字 or carries a dated source line. |

| 9 | Every episode ends with a blank version (a worksheet slide or a question) and a save prompt, because saves are the metric (`portfolio.md` 6.1). The Threads version ends with a question, never an instruction to follow or tag. |

## 2. The shared example world: "Form 0"

To make the series recognisable and to cut build time, every episode reuses one faceless dress form and one small set of generic flats. Changing a colour is the lesson; **only one thing changes per episode**, and the change is shown. These are teaching colours, not products, and no flat is ever described as a real item.

| Object | Fixed design (hex are guidance for whoever draws it; Claude draws it as SVG, decision D3) | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Dress form 人台 | Faceless, headless, neutral warm-grey form on a plain stand (one SVG symbol, reused) | All episodes |
| Open jacket flat | Warm stone `#B8AEA2`, plain buttons, side pockets | A #01, B1 #01 |
| Crew-neck top flat | Cream `#F4E8DF` | A #01, #02, B1 |
| Trousers flat | Charcoal `#3A3735`, straight and wide | A #01, B1, B2 |
| Scarf flat | Persimmon `#CD8B6A`, narrow, plain | A #01 |
| Accent set for "turn one step" | Wheel positions 1 to 4 and 12: dusty red `#C27F80`, red-orange `#C58479`, persimmon `#CD8B6A`, golden `#D2AE72`, red-violet `#B58AA0` | A |
| Neutral set | Warm stone `#B8AEA2`, camel, oat, cool slate grey `#9A9EA3` | A #02 |

The flats follow Lesson Kit v0 (`calendar-30d.md` 7.2, drawn by Claude as SVG and reviewed by the operator and the wife): no logo, tag, label, pattern, stripe or recognisable branded silhouette. If Form 0 changes, bump it to "Form 0 v2" in this table and note it in the calendar.

**Reel template (20 to 40 s, 9:16, captions only, no synthetic voice; NOT scheduled in the 30 days; decision D6 conditions apply):** 0 to 3 s hook (H03 is the model); about 6 s the dress form with the main colour; 10 to 15 s the secondary and accent colours arrive one beat each; about 5 s the turn (the wheel highlights one step and the accent changes); last 3 s the card with the studio line and the colour footer. The identity line appears only in the account's first Reel. Music only from the platform's own library, and only after gate G13 is checked. The still face may appear on the end card. Decision D6 (2026-10-12) closed calendar gate 15 and would also allow a slow pan or zoom of the still elsewhere in the clip; this template keeps the end card only, which is stricter than D6, and a human may relax that. Whatever the placement: platform AI label on; badge 「AI 生成｜虛擬角色」 on every frame for the whole clip; no blinking, lip-sync or morphing of the face; no synthetic voice (captions or human-recorded sound only). Claude cannot edit video, so the cut, timing and any recorded sound are a human job; Claude can supply the SVG frames (diagram beats, captions, end card) as PNG files.

**Source rule:** a labelled rule of thumb needs no outside source (NO FACTUAL CLAIM). A definition such as 類似色 or 互補色 shows the colour-theory reference (bible G7) on the footer slide. Hue positions are read from the hex values in the SVG source file.

## 4. Series B: 三步整理 (Three-step round-up), pillars P2 and P3

Two variants share one skeleton: **an ordered list or a checklist of exactly three items**.

| | B1 三層順序 (three layers) | B2 三關檢查 (three checks) |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar | P2 氣候穿搭 | P3 場合穿搭 |
| Promise | The inner, middle and outer layer are chosen in an order, by visible area; a dated weather panel shows which city and month the example is about | Three checks before an event: the occasion (the host's request), the colour, the shoes; the colour taboos are explained plainly |
| Source | The weather agency's own statistics page (gate 12); the three-layer order is labelled **the studio's framework, not a standard** | General etiquette (一般禮儀整理，請以主辦方的要求為準); a named, dated public source for any "customary" claim, or the wife's sign-off phrased as 「常見的做法」; a fashion contact sanity-checks (gate 16) |
| Never | Health, cold-prevention, sunburn, rash or sweat wording; fabric-performance words; "this will keep you warm"; a forecast; a scooter scene; a number typed from memory | All-white or all-black celebration outfits; "everyone does X"; funeral, temple or ritual attire; lucky colours; sets of four; gift framing of umbrella, shoes, clock, fan, scissors or handkerchief |

**Instagram carousel template (7 slides, 4:5):**

| Slide | B1 content | B2 content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover | The situation in 8 to 20 characters (a season, a room) + series chip; cut-out | The event in 8 to 20 characters + series chip; cut-out |
| 2 The three | 內層｜中層｜外層 as three flats numbered 1 to 3 | The three checks as three numbered rows with icons |
| 3 Why this order | Area logic: outer layer shows the most and sets the main colour (rule of thumb) | Why each check matters, one line each; the host's request prevails |
| 4 Data or taboo | **Dated weather panel** (city, month, statistic, source line, capture date; not a forecast) | The colour-taboo slide: why all-white and all-black are commonly avoided at celebrations (the wife's wording; a source or her sign-off) |
| 5 Illustrations | Two small dress forms | Three example outfits, each with a second colour |
| 6 Your turn | A blank order to fill | A blank checklist |
| 7 Footers | Climate source line, studio line, AI line, colour footer | Etiquette line, studio line, AI line, colour footer |

**Threads template:** a question about a habit (H14 is the model) or a three-item card.

**Reel template (NOT scheduled in the 30 days):** 0 to 3 s hook; one beat per item (three beats); last card with the source line. No scene of her in rain, no umbrella, no street. Plates (office, MRT exit, cinema, banquet hall) are drawn as SVG and never contain her (addendum 5.5). The D6 conditions above apply if a still of her is used on the end card.

## 5. Series C: 一詞看懂 (One word, understood), pillar P4

| | |
|---|---|
| Promise | One term from a product page (a measurement word, a label symbol, a colour name, a trend report), defined in plain words with a **blank template**, so a viewer can read any brand's page without the persona recommending anything. |
| Voice | Plain, definitional, a little dry: 「平量＝…」, 「圍度＝…」. |
| Source | A neutral public definition source (title, URL, date read) recorded by the operator, or a clearly marked example (範例數字，非真實商品). Which care-label or composition standard applies in Taiwan is *unverified* (bible open item 26). |

**Instagram carousel template (7 slides, 4:5):**

| Slide | Content | Reusable element |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover | The term in 「 」, 8 to 20 characters; series chip 「一詞看懂 #NN」; cut-out | Cover template |
| 2 The word | The definition and a generic flat with the mark or symbol drawn on it | Flat component |
| 3 The relation or the example | The arithmetic or the example (範例數字，非真實商品) | Bar component |
| 4 Blank template | A blank sheet to fill with the viewer's own garment's numbers | Worksheet block |
| 5 Check list | Three things to check on any chart or label | Three-row component |
| 6 Steps | Three steps to use it (numbers only; no body, no tape measure on a person) | Three-step strip |
| 7 Footers | Size and fit footer, example-number tag, definition source line, studio line, AI line | Footer block |

**Threads template:** a label card (the term, the two-line definition, the example) with no character panel; first line 「…圖為示意」.

**Monthly trend variant 「報導放上色環」 (at most once a month; first in January, after the weekly trend check has run for four weeks):** the post reports what **one named public source** says about a season's colours (a published show page, a lookbook page or a trend-forecast press release), places the colours it names on the wheel as swatches drawn as SVG, and says 「報導指出」, never 「會流行」. It shows no brand product, photograph or logo (swatches are drawn from hex values the operator chooses, never cropped or colour-picked from the source), quotes at most one sentence, gives the source name and date, and is sanity-checked by a fashion contact (bible G10). Protocol and template: `trend-check.md` section 6.

**Never in this series:** a real size chart, a real care label or a real brand page; a number typed from memory; a fit, comfort or "true to size" statement; care advice in her own voice; "fabric X is breathable"; a price; a named brand's colour name as if universal.

## 6. How the series rotate

- Over any week: one P1 or P4 post for the formula audience, one P2 or P3 post for the Taiwan-identity audience, and the Threads versions a day later.
- After OPS-D1 the 30 days hold 3 Series A episodes (2026-11-18, 11-23, 12-09), 4 Series B episodes (11-20, 12-02, 12-07, 12-14) and 2 Series C episodes (11-25, 12-04): 9 masters, 18 posts. The gap in Series A between 11-23 and 12-09 is the cost of deferring M5. *Assumption*; calibrate against saves and shares per reach at the week-6 review (`portfolio.md` 6.1).
- If one series' saves and shares per reach are clearly below the others after 8 posts per platform (the portfolio's minimum sample), run the Fix experiment of `portfolio.md` 6.3 on that series first (one variable for 4 weeks).

## Part B. The page for a fashion contact (send-ready only after the gates)

### B1. What this is

**Plainspoken Studio** (明說角色工作室, name provisional) makes AI-generated virtual characters that are always shown as what they are. We are exploring one service for fashion brands: **disclosed virtual-model lookbook creative built from your own product photographs**. The image is clearly labelled as an AI-generated illustration; the character has not worn, tried or tested anything; and nothing in it makes a claim about fit, comfort, body shape or results.

We are at the start. There is no past work to show and no client list. A first sample is offered free and privately so that you can judge the result before anything else is discussed.

### B2. What you give us, what we make, what you approve

| You give | We make | You approve |
|---|---|---|
| One or two products, as product-only photographs (flat lay, ghost mannequin or hanger) with a written usage licence; the official colour name, composition and size chart text you want quoted | Up to three still images of an original AI-generated character shown with the garment, plus alt text, in 4:5 and 1:1 (*proposed scope, assumption*) | **Every image, in writing, before any use.** We reject any output that changes a garment detail (collar, buttons, seams, pattern, colour), and you can reject any image for any reason |

### B3. The labelled-illustration rules (虛擬試穿示意)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Every image in which the character appears in your garment carries a persistent on-image label** 「虛擬試穿示意」 and 「AI 生成示意，角色未實際穿著」, typeset by a person (never generated), large enough to read on a phone and placed so that a square crop cannot remove it. |
| 2 | **It is built only from your product-only photographs.** No photograph of a real person is ever used as a reference. The character's face is the studio's own original design, with a recorded origin, and is never modelled on a real person or a named model. |
| 3 | **Garment fidelity.** The studio compares each image with your photograph at 200% before showing it to you. If a collar, button, seam, pattern or colour differs, the image is regenerated or dropped. We do not invent, add or "improve" garment details. Whether the tools can hold fidelity is **untested**; the first sample is the test. |
| 4 | **Colour.** Screens shift colour. The caption quotes your official colour name and carries the line 「螢幕有色差，實際顏色以品牌提供的資訊為準。」 We do not colour-correct a garment to look better. |
| 5 | **No fit, comfort, size, body, skin, slimming or health claims.** Fabric-performance words appear only as your own published specification, quoted and dated, and you warrant them. |
| 6 | **No testimonial.** The character is never described as having worn, tried, liked or tested the garment. Captions use the words 搭配示意 and 虛擬試穿示意, never 「今天穿」. |
| 7 | **Disclosure in four places** on anything the studio publishes: the account bio, the first caption line (「【AI 虛擬角色】…」), the on-image badge, and the platform's own AI label. |
| 8 | **Paid or collaborative content is labelled as such.** If the content is posted on a studio character's account it carries 「【合作內容】」 or 「【業配】」 on the first line and the platform's paid-partnership tool. Whether a free sample posted publicly counts as an advertisement is **unverified** and is a question for a Taiwanese lawyer; the first sample is therefore **not posted**. |
| 9 | **Stills only at first.** No video, voice or lip-sync for a photoreal character until a separate review exists. |
| 10 | **No logo is generated.** If a garment carries a mark, either the garment is chosen without one, or the mark is placed by a person from artwork you supply, after your approval. |
| 11 | **No resemblance warranty beyond origin.** The studio warrants how the face was made and checked, and makes no warranty about chance resemblance; you may not ask for resemblance to any real person. |

### B4. What we will not do

Make a claim of fit, comfort, slimming, brightening or any skin or body effect; say a character wore or liked a garment; resemble a named model, actress or creator; use a real model's photograph or voice; hide or shrink the AI label or the paid-content label; use seeded likes, friends' comments or engagement groups; compare your product with a named competitor; or promise views, reach, followers or sales.

### B5. How a first sample would work (proposed; *assumption*)

1. A short written scope: the products, the number of stills, two rounds of changes, private delivery, no obligation on either side.
2. You send the product-only photographs and the label text. We confirm in writing what we will use them for (a review sample only).
3. We draft one to three stills and run the fidelity check. We tell you honestly if the tools cannot hold your garment's details.
4. You approve, change or reject each image. Nothing is posted anywhere.
5. We talk about whether any paid work makes sense. Terms (licence, usage period, approvals, payment, who signs) are settled in a written contract after the studio's registration is complete. No price is given here: **no Taiwan price benchmark was found, and the studio quotes after scoping.**

### B6. What we are not claiming

No client, result, engagement figure or past campaign is claimed, because none exists. The studio's own characters are new and have no audience yet. We are not a substitute for human models where a brand needs verifiable real wearers; some brands will reasonably prefer that, and this service is not for them.

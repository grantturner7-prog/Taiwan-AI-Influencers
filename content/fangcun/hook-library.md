# 方寸 (fangcun) hook library

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5).** 46 hooks for the first 30 days and the seasons after. Every zh-TW hook is an AI draft for the wife; none has been approved or used publicly. Hooks are written to the persona bible (`personas/fangcun/bible.md` sections 2.6, 3 and 6) and the photoreal addendum (`personas/fangcun/photoreal-addendum.md`, which wins where they differ). Evidence labels: *assumption* = a planning figure to calibrate; *unverified* = not checked against a primary source; *computed* = calculated in this session.

## 1. What a 方寸 hook is, and the rules every hook obeys

A hook is the first line a viewer meets: an Instagram cover line, a Reel or Short opener on screen, or the first line of a comment thread. Every hook below was written against these rules (all from the bible and the ground rules; the numbers are *assumptions* to calibrate):

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At most 20 characters, counting punctuation and spaces (the longest hook in this file is 19 characters) | Bible 3.1; readable on a phone cover |
| 2 | One idea, one line; a question, a number or a plain statement | Bible 3.1 |
| 3 | Ask or state something the post answers by arithmetic, a checklist or a dated source. No hook promises a result | Ground rules 4 and 6 |
| 4 | Never claim lived experience. No 我用過, 我家, 我租的, 我量過, 開箱, 實測, 愛用, 改造, 好用 (as her own verdict), 神器, 必買, 推薦 as a personal endorsement, 踩雷 | Ground rule 4; bible 3.2 and 3.4 |
| 5 | The only "I" hooks are non-experience statements about the design (H37 to H42). Hands, the belt and the lack of a home are design facts, never memories | Bible 2.6; addendum 12.2 |
| 6 | No scarcity or pressure wording (限時, 手刀, 最後一天, 不看會後悔), no price, discount or sale claims, no 最好 / 第一名 / 最划算 | Bible 2.4, 7.2 item 20 |
| 7 | No health, mould, allergy, sleep, pest or air-quality wording; no lease, deposit, landlord, property-investment or finance wording | Ground rule 6; bible 7.2 |
| 8 | No politics, election or party-colour hooks; no 雙11 hook (2026-11-11 is before W0); a 雙12 hook only in the window shown in H31 | Ground rule 6; `research/culture.md` 4 and 5 |
| 9 | Taiwan vocabulary: 影片, 資訊, 捲尺, 公分, 公尺, 平方公尺, 坪, 冰箱, 電鍋, 玄關, 套房. No 親, 寶子, 家人們, no 注音文 | Bible 3.3; `culture.md` 3.1 |
| 10 | Pronouns: 我, 你, the name 方寸, or 「這個角色」. Never 他 or 她 | Bible 2.1 item 7; addendum 2 and 12 item 1 (wife rules) |
| 11 | Numbers inside a hook are arithmetic or 示意數字. A hook never states a "standard" size or clearance | Bible 7.2 item 12 |
| 12 | The method phrase 先算 / 先量 (and 先看) is the studio's phrase: use it in at most 1 hook in 4 (*assumption*, from `personas/README.md` V3) so the feed does not read as one template | `personas/README.md` 6 |

## 5. Testing hooks without fooling ourselves

*assumption:* the first 6 weeks produce about 8 organic posts per platform (`portfolio.md` 6.1), which is far too few to rank 46 hooks. So:

1. Record the hook ID and pattern in the analytics tracker (Phase 7) with every post. Do not change anything else about a post to "test" a hook.
2. At the week-6 review (2026-12-28, *assumption*) read the saves and shares per reach by pattern **as a direction only**, not as a result. Three or more posts per pattern are needed before the table says anything.
3. Never use the numbers to justify buying or arranging engagement (Ground rule 7); never write a hook to the metric if it breaks a rule in section 1.
4. If a pattern earns comments that ask for personal verdicts ("which one is better?"), that is a signal to answer with a neutral comparison (`reply-guidelines.md`), not to change the persona.

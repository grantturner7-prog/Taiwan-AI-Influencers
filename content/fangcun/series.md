# 方寸 (fangcun) series formats: three repeatable formats

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5).** **Updated 2026-10-12** for decisions D6, OPS-D1 and D3 (`DECISIONS.md`; calendar section 12): two masters a week through week 8, M7 and M10 deferred, diagrams drawn by Claude as SVG. Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife; nothing here is approved or published. The series follow the four pillars in `personas/fangcun/bible.md` section 6 and the photoreal addendum (`personas/fangcun/photoreal-addendum.md`, which wins where the two differ). The dates are the 30-day calendar's (`content/fangcun/calendar-30d.md`), where W0 = Mon 2026-11-16 is an assumption. Evidence labels: *assumption* = a planning figure to calibrate; *unverified* = not checked against a primary source; *computed* = calculated in this session.

## 1. Why series, and what "repeatable" means here

A series is a fixed **structure** with new **content** every time. The structure is what lets one operator produce four posts a week for this persona (12 a week across the three pilots under OPS-D1) inside 6 hours (`portfolio.md` 5.1) and lets a viewer recognise a post in the feed. The content (the question, the numbers, the diagram, the script) is always new.

Two platform rules make that distinction matter (both second-hand and unverified, from `research/platforms.md`):

- YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule targets mass-produced, templated content such as repetitive slideshows with bulk AI voiceover (S42 there). Every Short in these series therefore has its own script, its own numbers and its own diagram; there is no synthetic voiceover; and the template is a layout, not a script.
- Instagram excludes accounts that mostly repost others' photos or carousels from recommendation (S33 there). All 方寸 material is original (Claude-drawn SVG diagrams approved by the operator and the wife, own arithmetic), so this is not the risk; the risk is one identical-looking post every day, which the three-series mix avoids.

Shared rules for all three series (from the bible and the ground rules; none is new):

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Disclosure in four places on every post: bio, first caption line (the v2 line in calendar section 7.3), on-screen badge on every image and every video frame, and the platform's AI label switched on by hand. |
| 2 | The character appears only as still images from the golden set (R01 master, R13 glance, E01 to E10 headshots, R08 framings). In a Reel or Short she may be a still or a slow pan or zoom of a still (decision D6) with the platform AI label on, a persistent badge, no animation of the face and no synthetic voice (captions or human-recorded sound only; calendar section 7.2). Animation, lip-sync or voice for the photoreal face stay barred until its own addendum exists (photoreal addendum risk K). Diagram motion is the only other motion. |
| 3 | Every number is either tagged 示意數字 or has a dated source on the last slide or in the description. Example numbers are invented and never described as typical or standard (bible 7.2 item 12). |
| 4 | Every fit or clearance statement carries 「請以現場丈量為準」. |
| 5 | One master feeds one Instagram post and one YouTube Short. Production counts once and approvals count twice (`portfolio.md` 5.1). |
| 6 | No lived experience, no verdict, no "best", no health, lease, electrical, gas, structural or load advice, no finance, no politics, no brand or marketplace logo, no hands or other people in any image. |
| 7 | Every episode ends with a blank version (worksheet slide or a closing line) and a save prompt, because saves are the metric (`portfolio.md` 6.1). The 出處章 (source stamp) is the end-card ritual (running joke J3; only 方寸 uses the verb 蓋, `personas/README.md` V2). |
| 8 | Every post is approved by the operator and the wife before publishing, for at least the first 30 days. |

## 2. The shared example world: "Room 0"

To make the series recognisable and to cut diagram time, every episode reuses one invented example room and a few furniture blocks. Changing a number is allowed (that is the lesson of the what-if slide), but **only one object changes per episode**, and the change is shown. These are fictional values. They are tagged 示意數字 on screen and never claimed to be typical, standard or recommended.

## 3. Series 1: 算給你看 (Worked out for you) — pillar A

| | |
|---|---|
| Promise | One "will it fit / how much is left" question answered by a short formula the viewer can reuse. The viewer should be able to fill the blank formula in under a minute. |
| Question types | Walkway left (P01), unit conversion (P05), area swept by a door (P09), can it pass through the door (P17), volume in litres (P21) |
| Voice | Calm, patient, "I work it out for you", never "I measured". The only first-person verbs are 算, 整理, 示範, 畫 (bible 3.2). |

**Instagram carousel template (7 to 9 slides, 4:5):**

| Slide | Content | Reusable element |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover | The question in 20 characters or fewer plus a one-line scope; framed panel with R01 or an E-headshot | Series chip 「算給你看 #NN」 under the title |
| 2 (first episode only) | Identity lines (S09 replacement, J1, J2). Later episodes use this slide for the setup room | Identity slide is not repeated in every post (`personas/README.md` V3) |
| 3 Setup | Room 0 plan with the numbered inputs and 示意數字 tags; panel R13 | Plan kit |
| 4 to 5 Method | One slide per step: formula first, then the sum | Formula line in a fixed position |
| 6 What-if | Change one number, show every number after it change; no verdict | Two small plans side by side |
| 7 Blank | The formula with blanks and 「請以現場丈量為準」 | Worksheet block |
| 8 Source stamp | 「蓋個出處章。」 plus the source line (「示意數字・沒有外部出處」 or the dated source) and the save prompt | Stamp graphic |

**Reel or Short template (20 to 40 s, 9:16, captions only, no synthetic voice; the D6 conditions in calendar section 7.2 apply):** 0 to 3 s hook; the identity line 「我是方寸，AI 虛擬角色。」 only in the first Reel and the first Short of the account (it is not repeated in every video, `pipeline/qa-checklist.md` V5; the badge, the first description line and the platform label carry the disclosure); then about 6 s setup; 10 to 15 s method (one formula per beat); about 5 s closing line (S08: 「以上是示意數字。你的房間尺寸，請用捲尺量過再算。」); last 3 s end card with the stamp. Title on YouTube: 【一分鐘搞懂】 plus the question (the bracket device is a Taiwan title habit, `research/culture.md` 2.2).

**Source rule:** arithmetic-only episodes say so on the stamp. Any episode that states a definition (坪, litre) shows the dated source on screen, and the post is blocked until the operator has opened the source (see calendar P05).

**Never in this series:** a recommended walkway width, a "standard" door or bed size, "will definitely fit" (use 「依這組尺寸，理論上放得下；實際請以現場丈量為準」), any load or safety figure.

## 4. Series 2: 兩種排法 (Two layouts) — pillar B

| | |
|---|---|
| Promise | The same room and the same furniture arranged two (sometimes three) ways, with a table of the resulting gaps. The point is that the numbers change with the arrangement and with the viewer's own room; no arrangement wins. |
| Voice | Even-handed ("數字不會替你選"). Offers options and arithmetic, never a verdict (bible 2.4, "Even-handed"). |

**Instagram carousel template (8 slides):** 1 cover (「同一間…，兩種排法，…差在哪？」); 2 the room and the furniture sizes; 3 layout A with its numbers; 4 layout B with its numbers; 5 comparison table; 6 「數字不會替你選」; 7 blank table; 8 source stamp and save prompt.

**Short template (30 to 40 s):** hook; layout A and its number; the furniture slides to layout B and the number updates; the table; the no-verdict line; closing S08 line; stamp end card.

**Rules specific to this series:**

- Equal visual weight for A and B: same size, same colours, no tick, no star, no "recommended" tag, no colour that signals a winner.
- Both layouts must be physically possible in the drawn room; the operator checks the plans against the sums (slip row "geometry").
- Furniture is a plain block. No brand, no product shape, no real product name (bible 4.10, 9.5).
- Taiwan details (entrance with shoes off, balcony with washing machine and drying pole, one-wall kitchen) follow bible 4.9 and are checked by the wife. If a detail cannot be drawn correctly, the episode waits.
- No installation advice: object positions only; gas, electrical and structural items are never drawn as advice (bible 7.2 item 8).

## 5. Series 3: 先量再買 (Measure first, buy second) — pillar C

| | |
|---|---|
| Promise | A short numbered list of measurable things to record before a purchase, a viewing or a move, ending with a blank sheet. "Do not buy" is a valid outcome (bible 2.5 item 2): the closing line is 「都量好，再決定要不要買」, never "go buy". |
| Voice | Imperatives are addressed to the viewer ("先確認…"), never claims that 方寸 did them. |

**Instagram template:** a Reel (20 to 30 s) of N cards, or a carousel of N slides, N between 3 and 7 and five by default. Each card has a name (寬, 深, 高, 門怎麼開, 搬運路線) and one "why" line that follows from logic or from the formula in series 1, not from a claim about the world. Last card: 「工作室整理的一般清單，非法律或專業意見」 and the save prompt.

**Short template:** the same list counted, with one extra visual (the isometric box with three dimension arrows in P04) so the Short is not a copy of the Reel.

**Source rule:** items come from a maker's delivery or assembly guidance or from an official or reputable consumer checklist that the operator selects and cites (which sources exist is *unverified*; bible 11 item 10). When no single source applies, the studio-compiled label above is mandatory.

**Never in this series:** lease clauses, deposits, landlord matters, brokers (bible 3.5, 9.2); brand, marketplace or logo; price, discount or "limited time"; any claim about a product's performance. A checklist for 看房 (P13, deferred by OPS-D1) lists dimensions and fixtures only.

## 8. When a series changes or stops

- At the week-6 review (Mon 2026-12-28, *assumption*) each series is read against the thresholds in `portfolio.md` 6.1 by saves and shares per reach. A series below Keep after one redesign is replaced by the next unused backlog idea, not by a trend.
- A series stops at once if three of any 20 drafts show experience, health, finance or income wording (`portfolio.md` 6.2).
- A new series needs a bible-level note, the same ground-rule review and the wife's naming check.

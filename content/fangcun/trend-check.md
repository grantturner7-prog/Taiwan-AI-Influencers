# 方寸 (fangcun) weekly trend-check routine

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5).** A 30-minute weekly routine that uses only public pages read by hand. Nothing here has been run; no page named below was opened in this session (web access was limited, so every statement about what a page shows or requires is *unverified* until the operator tries it). Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife. Evidence labels: *assumption* = a planning figure to calibrate; *unverified* = not checked against a primary source.

## 1. What the routine is for, and what it is not for

**For:** picking next week's calendar hook (a season, a shopping date, a search term people use), catching Taiwan phrasing that has aged or turned cringe, spotting a platform-policy change early, and stopping a post that lands on a bad day.

**Not for:** copying a viral format, chasing a meme, or deciding what the persona "thinks". A trend never overrides the pillars (`personas/fangcun/bible.md` section 6). The persona has no lived experience, so most social trends (day-in-my-life, GRWM, "what I bought", room tours, before and after) are closed to it by Ground rule 4 (`research/culture.md` 2.3). The default outcome of a trend check is **no change to next week's masters**.

## 2. Hard limits (the ground rules applied to research)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Public pages only, read by a person.** Only pages that open without logging in, plus the platforms' own official dashboards for the studio's own accounts. If a page asks for a login to show content, skip it. |
| 2 | **No scraping and no automation.** No scripts, no browser extensions that collect content, no bulk export, no API use for other people's content, no screenshots harvested at scale. Copy at most a short phrase into the trend note with the page name and the date. |
| 3 | **No login-wall browsing as research.** Do not sign in to the persona accounts to browse other accounts or other people's posts for research. The persona accounts are used to publish, to read the studio's own insights and to answer comments (`reply-guidelines.md`). The operator's and the wife's personal accounts must not like, comment on, share, follow or save persona posts (`portfolio.md` 3.3 item 8). |
| 4 | **Never touch another persona's accounts or posts** as a persona, and never use another studio persona as a "trend source" (`portfolio.md` 3.3). |
| 5 | **No personal data in the note.** Names, handles and photos of private individuals are not recorded; a commenter's words are paraphrased, never pasted with a handle. |
| 6 | **Evidence rule.** Every item in the note carries the page name and the access date. If it cannot be checked, write "unverified". Never write an invented number. |
| 7 | **No fake popularity.** Nothing from the check is ever used to buy, arrange or seed engagement (Ground rule 7). |

## 7. Filters: what a trend may become

A trend is used only if it passes **all four** filters; otherwise it is logged as "not used" with the reason.

| Filter | Question | Fail example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Ground rules | Does it need a lived experience, a result claim, a health, finance or politics angle, a hand or a second person in the picture, or a brand? | "Room tour", "what I bought on 雙11", "dehumidifier test" |
| 2 Pillar fit | Does it fit A (arithmetic), B (two layouts), C (measure first) or D (a dated hook)? | A dance sound with no measurable content |
| 3 Calendar and taboo | Is the day clear (section 4)? No gift taboo, no mourning colours, no number four as a price or count? | A post on polling day |
| 4 Source | Can every number be computed or sourced with a date before the post? | A "standard size" claim without a source |

| Trend type | Allowed action |
|---|---|
| A rising everyday search (for example moving, or a seasonal storage question) | Plan a pillar post with its own arithmetic, at the next free slot |
| A shopping date (雙12, 週年慶, 618) | A planning checklist posted days before, with no price or sale wording and no brand |
| A new phrase from the wife's scan | Use it only after she rules, and never as a hook with a personal claim |
| A format that needs a camera on a person or a room tour | Not used |
| A controversy about AI images, labels or a brand's AI mistake | Not commented on by the persona. The operator reads it for policy risk only |
| A political, health or finance topic | Not used. If it appears in comments, hide it (`reply-guidelines.md`) |

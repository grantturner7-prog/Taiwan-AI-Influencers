Design choices (*assumption*; all to be tested, not defended):

- **The Short follows the Instagram post by one day** from the same master. Production is counted once and approvals twice (`portfolio.md` 5.1). The pair is also the test for hypothesis H3 (Instagram carousel against YouTube Shorts for N9, `portfolio.md` 4.2): same idea, two platforms.
- **Staggering with the other Instagram pilot (色環).** The studio rule is no coordinated launch on the same day and no two personas posting in the same hour (`portfolio.md` 3.3 item 6). This calendar proposes that 方寸 launches on Instagram on Mon 2026-11-16 and keeps Mon and Thu 11:30 to 13:30 clear for itself; 色環's calendar should launch on a different day (for example Wed 2026-11-18) and post at other hours. **This is a proposal; the other streams were written in parallel and the operator must reconcile the three calendars** (open item 5).
- **Weekends only from week 3,** because a saved checklist or worksheet is a weekend job for a renter. No weekend post is scheduled in weeks 1 and 2.
- **Rest days are not empty:** they carry the comment batches (`reply-guidelines.md`), the Friday trend check (`trend-check.md`) and the weekly report.
- **Pause switch** (`portfolio.md` 5.5): a disaster, a typhoon day or a public tragedy unschedules everything.

# 步驟 (buzhou) hook library

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5, content).** 41 hooks for the YouTube Shorts and Threads posts of 步驟. Every zh-TW line is an AI draft for the wife to review. No hook has been tested with an audience: nothing here says that any hook works. The hooks used in the first four masters are marked in the table and in section 4.

## 1. What a 步驟 hook is, and the rules every hook obeys

A hook is the first thing a viewer sees: the first 2 seconds of a Short (on-screen caption) or the first line of a Threads post after the disclosure line.

1. **It states a method, a place on a page, or a neutral question.** It never states a result, a verdict on a tool, or an experience (Ground rule 4).
2. **It is 20 full-width characters or fewer**, punctuation included, and ends with a full stop or a question mark. The longest in the table is 17.
3. **No experience, hype, income or urgency words** (bible 3.4): none of 實測, 親測, 試用, 好用, 神器, 必裝, 最強, 效率, 輕鬆, 一鍵, 推薦, 副業, 賺錢, 限時, 馬上, 立刻, and no unsourced audience line ("很多人", "最多人", a percentage).
4. **No personal request.** A hook never asks the viewer to paste, send or upload anything.
5. **Taiwan vocabulary** (bible 3.3): 資料, 檔案, 上傳, 軟體, 介面, 設定, 提示詞 (the wife rules against 指令), 匯出, 帳單.
6. **The phrase 「先看 X」 is the studio's method phrase** and 步驟 keeps it for dates and limits (`personas/README.md` V3): as the opening hook on **at most 1 post in 4**, so the feed does not read as one template. The ritual line 「第 0 步，先看日期。」 itself sits in scene 3 of every Short (bible joke J1); it is the only place the phrase repeats.
7. **The identity line 「我是步驟，AI 虛擬角色。」 is not a hook.** It belongs on the pinned post and the first launch Short only (V3). The identity hooks H25 and H26 are for that use.
8. **Placeholders `{...}`** are filled from the source slip before use. A hook with a brace in it must not be published (none of the hooks below has one).
9. **One hook, one idea, one master.** The Short and its Threads post use the same hook, so the pair is a single test.

Legend: P1 功能三步驟, P2 官方對照表, P3 提示詞範本, P4 用之前先看; YT = YouTube Shorts, TH = Threads; "Chars" = full-width character count of the hook line.

## 3. Hook patterns banned for 步驟, with the reason

| Banned pattern | Example (do not write) | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hype about a tool | 「這個 AI 工具太神了」「史上最強」 | Superlative and recommendation; Ground rule 4 and bible 3.4 |
| Experience | 「我用了三個月」「我試了 10 個工具」「實測」 | A virtual character has not used anything (Ground rule 4) |
| Income or side hustle | 「用 AI 月入…」「副業」「被動收入」 | N7 bar (`portfolio.md` 2C); fraud adjacency; Ground rule 6 |
| Fear or urgency | 「再不學就被取代」「限時」「馬上學」 | Bible 3.4; pressure copy |
| Unsourced audience claims | 「很多人都不知道」「99% 的人不會」 | Invents an audience history or an unsourced number |
| Ease or speed promises | 「一鍵搞定」「3 分鐘學會」「效率翻倍」「輕鬆」 | A result claim the character cannot have |
| Asking for personal data | 「把你的履歷貼給 AI」「截圖傳給我」 | N7 tripwire: the persona never asks viewers for personal data |
| Engagement bait | 「留言 +1 我私訊你」「按讚領範本」 | Ground rule 7; no giveaways |
| Bypass and evasion | 「免費用到爽」「繞過限制」「降 AI 率」 | Ground rule 9 and bible 7.2 (no bypass, no detector evasion) |
| Politics and elections | 「AI 幫你寫演講稿」「選情」 | Ground rule 6; the election window |
| Naming a winner or a loser | 「X 完勝 Y」「別再用 Z」 | A verdict on a named product; comparative-advertising limits *unverified* |
| Money, health, legal advice from AI | 「問 AI 買哪支股票」「用 AI 看診」 | Ground rule 6 |
| Clickbait cliff-hangers | 「最後一步你一定想不到」 | Manipulative; promises a reveal the character cannot own |
| Mainland-style hooks | 「干貨」「家人們」「必看」 | Mainland phrasing is called out fast (BRIEF section 5); 必看 is also urgency |
| First-person test claims | 「我發現…」 (after use), 「我整理了一晚」 | Invents an experience; use 「我整理」 only for what the studio compiled from public pages |

## 5. Testing hooks without fooling ourselves

1. **One variable at a time.** Change the hook only; keep pillar, length, thumbnail frame and posting slot. With 2 to 3 posts a week per platform there is no statistical power in 30 days: treat any comparison as a prompt for the week-8 review, not a finding.
2. **Compare like with like.** A Short is compared with Shorts, a Threads post with Threads posts. Platforms are not compared with each other, except with the common metric in `portfolio.md` 6.1.
3. **No seeded engagement** (Ground rule 7): no likes, comments or shares from the operator's or wife's personal accounts, no pods, no bought views, to rescue a hook.
4. **Read the comments before the numbers.** A hook that draws 是真人嗎 questions, confusion about what the video is, or a request for personal help is a weaker hook whatever the view count.
5. **Log every test** in the weekly log (template below). Report nothing as "worked" before the minimum sample (8 organic posts per platform in the window).
6. **Retire** a hook that draws a correction, a confusion cluster or a complaint about a promise it did not keep.

## 6. Checklist for the wife (hooks only)

Use the reply format APPROVE / SMALL FIX / REJECT with the codes V vocabulary, T tone, E experience claim, R regulated topic, P politics, X culture (`research/culture.md` 8.1).

1. Natural Taiwan Mandarin, not translation-ese? (H05 and H17 especially; H23 on 指令 against 提示詞.)
2. Any word that sounds mainland, stiff, or like an advertisement (工商味)? Mark live, dated or wrong.
3. 先別轉貼 / 別漏看 / 先停三秒: friendly or bossy?
4. 保存期限 (H02): does it read as the food-packaging joke it is meant to be, or as odd?
5. H05 (the pun on 步驟) and H27 (站在角落): charming or forced?
6. H15 assumes a problem; H34 and H35 touch a data practice or advice: keep, soften or drop?
7. H41 and any 雙12 hook: pushing shopping, or a neutral reading guide?
8. Is any hook an experience claim, a result promise or a fear line in your ear, even without the banned words?
9. Do two hooks sound like the same hook in different words? Mark duplicates.
10. For the Threads questions (H10, H28, H29, H30): do they sound like a real person asking, or like bait?

# 步驟 (buzhou) series formats: three repeatable formats

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5, content).** Three formats that every post of 步驟 uses, so that each new post needs a new topic and a new recording but not a new design. Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife to review. Nothing here has been produced or tested. Production times are *assumptions*; the first four masters are the measurement.

## 1. Why series, and what "repeatable" means here

- **What repeats:** the structure, the label system (錄製條件, 資料來源, 示範輸出, the badge), the corner picture, the 第 0 步 date line and the closing line. These are what a viewer learns to read, and they are the same checks the operator makes every time.
- **What never repeats:** the script, the recording, the quoted lines and the source. YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule targets mass-produced, templated content with bulk automated voiceover (`research/platforms.md` 4.3, secondary and unverified). 步驟 has no voice, and each video is its own dated recording and its own text, so a repeated structure is not a repeated video. The operator still keeps this check: if two Shorts could swap their recordings and nobody would notice, one of them should not exist.
- **Three formats, four pillars.** 「三步驟」 carries P1 功能三步驟. 「對照表」 carries P2 官方對照表. 「範本清單」 carries P3 提示詞範本 and P4 用之前先看 (a template card and a checklist card are built the same way). Pillar names are the bible's (section 6).
- **Weekly mix** (bible section 6): in a 3-master week, one P1, one P2, one P3; P4 replaces the P3 once in three weeks. In a 2-master week, two of the three series.
- **Names to confirm with the wife:** 「三步驟」 (clear), 「對照表」 (clear), 「範本清單」 (is 清單 natural beside 範本, and does the name read as a mere list?). 「小抄」 was rejected because it also means exam crib notes (cheating), which this channel must never suggest.

## 2. The shared example world: 示範小組

Every demo uses invented sample files from **Sample Kit v0 in `calendar-30d.md` section 7.7**: a sample letter, a three-page sample set of meeting minutes, a template and fixed prompts, all about one fictional workshop. There is one copy of the kit, so the digits and weekdays checked in the source slips are the same everywhere.

Rules for adding to the kit:

1. Invented people are roles (同事甲, 同事乙, 主管丙), never names, and no company, school, address, product or event is real.
2. Every file says on its face that it is a sample with fictional content (「示範用，內容純屬虛構」).
3. Dates fall in the coming weeks and carry the right weekday (compute them); numbers are small and checkable by eye.
4. No money, health, law, politics, election, scam or religion in any sample. No sample that looks like an official notice.
5. Each new item gets a letter, a list of the digits to check in any output, and the wife's read before first use.
6. An invented example is never described as typical, standard or recommended.

## 3. Series 1: 三步驟 (Feature in three steps), pillar P1

- **Promise to the viewer (no result):** "Here is where one official feature is, in three steps, with the page and the date it comes from."
- **Used in the 30 days:** M2 (S2/T2), M5, M8, M11. Backlog in section 9.
- **Who it serves (hypothesis):** a working adult or student searching for how to find one setting. The format is search-led (title carries the tool, the feature and the month).

**Structure of the Short (target 48 to 52 seconds; times are *assumption*)**

| Time | Beat | Fixed element |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Hook caption | One line from the hook library; the corner picture on E04 or E06 |
| 0:03 to 0:08 | 第 0 步，先看日期。 | Date stamp pulses once; the 錄製條件 strip is visible from here to the end |
| 0:08 to 0:38 | Steps 1 to 3, about 10 seconds each | A step block (a large numeral), the real recording, a callout, and one dated 資料來源 label in the step that makes a factual claim |
| 0:38 to 0:46 | The caveat | 「示範輸出，不代表每次相同。」 where an output is shown; 「以官方頁面為準。」 |
| 0:46 to 0:50 | The closer | 「這支影片有保存期限。資料日期在畫面上方。」 (joke J4) |

**Threads counterpart:** the three steps as numbered text lines, one dated sentence naming the page and the date, one caveat line, and 「錄影版會發布在 YouTube 頻道「步驟」。」 No link inside the text (a link, if wanted, is added by the operator as a reply; nothing in the pilot is an affiliate link).

**What the operator records:** the feature on the live app, on a studio test account, with the real plan and version on the strip; the official help page that describes it, with the line quoted; one run of the feature with sample data.

**What the operator verifies:** the feature exists on the recording day; each step matches what the page says; every limit, name and menu word on screen is copied from the page, not retyped; the plan used is the one on the strip.

**Not in this format:** a tutorial for a bypass, a paywall workaround, a jailbreak, an automation that breaks a tool's terms, a deepfake or voice-cloning tool, or anything that needs a viewer's personal data.

## 4. Series 2: 對照表 (Official comparison table), pillar P2

- **Promise to the viewer (no result):** "Here is what each official page says, side by side or field by field, with its date. I do not pick one for you."
- **Used in the 30 days:** M4 (single-page reading variant), M7, M9. Backlog in section 9.
- **Two variants.** (a) **Reading variant**: one vendor page read by its fields (M4, M9). (b) **Quote variant**: two vendors' pages, one quoted line each, same topic (M7). The quote variant is held until the lawyer has answered the comparative-advertising question (calendar section 9, item 8); its fallback is the reading variant with one vendor.

**Structure of the Short (target 46 to 52 seconds)**

| Time | Beat | Fixed element |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Hook caption | e.g. 「價格頁這三格，別漏看。」 |
| 0:03 to 0:08 | 第 0 步，先看日期。 | Prices and limits change: the date is the first row of the table |
| 0:08 to 0:42 | Row by row (three rows) | The page is on screen; each row is a field with a question caption, the page's own words or figure, and a 資料來源 label |
| 0:42 to 0:46 | The refusal line | 「我不判斷哪個方案值得，只整理頁面寫了什麼。」 |
| 0:46 to 0:50 | The closer | 「價格以官方頁面當天顯示為準。這支影片有保存期限。」 |

**Table rules (all are Ground rule 4, 6 and 10 applied):**

1. **Quote, never paraphrase**, a price, a limit, a data-handling or deletion statement. Keep the page's currency, period and tax statement as written.
2. **No colour that says good or bad.** No green or red cells, ticks or crosses, stars, rankings or badges. Outlines are charcoal, text is charcoal on bone.
3. **No conversion and no arithmetic** on prices (no "per month if yearly", no exchange rate). If a conversion is the point, the page's own converted figure is quoted with its label.
4. **Vendor claims are attributed and dated** (「廠商宣稱…（日期）」). A claim is never shown as the character's finding.
5. **At most two vendors** in one table, same topic, same field order, same date. Never a "best" row, never a total.
6. **Rows follow the page's own order**, so the viewer can find them on the page.
7. **Say what is missing**: if a page does not state something, the row reads 「這頁沒寫。」, not a guess.

**Threads counterpart:** a dated text list or a typeset card (no person on the card, 「AI 生成｜示意圖」 badge), sources in the text.

**What the operator verifies:** each quoted figure against the page; the currency, period and tax words; the page's region and language; the date and time seen; that no promotion banner is zoomed on.

## 5. Series 3: 範本清單 (Template or checklist card), pillars P3 and P4

- **Promise to the viewer (no result):** "Here is one general text or checklist, written by the studio, with one recorded output and a reminder that outputs vary and need checking."
- **Used in the 30 days:** M1 (P4 checklist), M3 (P3 template), M6 (P3), M10 (P4). Backlog in section 9.
- **Two variants.** (a) **Template card (P3)**: a prompt template written by the studio, with its limits as three short lines, run on sample data. (b) **Checklist card (P4)**: three things to check before using or sharing, each tied to a page the viewer can open.

**Structure of the Short (target 48 to 54 seconds)**

| Time | Beat | Fixed element |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Hook caption | e.g. 「同一個提示詞，每次輸出可能不一樣。」 |
| 0:03 to 0:07 | 第 0 步，先看日期。 | |
| 0:07 to 0:13 | Step 1: no personal data | 「這封信是工作室寫的虛構範例。」 The sample is shown with its 虛構 note |
| 0:13 to 0:24 | Step 2: the card | The template lines appear one at a time, then are pasted into the tool (P3); or the checklist items appear one at a time over their pages (P4) |
| 0:24 to 0:36 | The output (P3) | 「示範輸出，第 N 次，共 M 次」, shown for the number of runs promised on screen and no fewer |
| 0:36 to 0:50 | Step 3: compare with the original | Highlights on dates, numbers and names in both; 「標示不確定，不代表內容正確。」 |
| 0:50 to 0:54 | The closer | 「這支影片有保存期限。範本通用，輸出依工具而不同。」 |

**Card rules:**

1. **The template text is original** and the studio's own (not copied from any prompt pack). It is shown in full on screen and in the description, so the viewer can copy it.
2. **No claim that a template gives better, faster or correct results.** The output is labelled 示範輸出; a request to "mark what is uncertain" does not make the output reliable, and the video says so.
3. **No topic that touches medical, legal, tax, investment, gambling, election, income or someone's private data.** The sample world is the fictional workshop.
4. **Never a way to hide AI use, beat a detector or cheat** (a school-facing template says "follow your school's rules").
5. **The checklist variant (P4) cites a page for every item** that states a fact about a tool or a law; where the topic touches the personal data law, the source is an official government page (unverified) and no legal advice is given.

**Threads counterpart:** the template itself as text (slots in 〔 〕, not braces) or the checklist as three lines, the caveat line, and the channel pointer.

**What the operator verifies:** the digits in every output against the sample; the run count shown equals the runs made; the template on screen equals the template in the description; any vendor statement quoted.

## 7. Production time per episode (*assumption*, to be measured in the first 30 days)

| Series | Recording and claims check (portfolio figure) | Edit once the editor project exists | Wife (review plus on-screen output) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 三步驟 | 20 min | 40 to 60 min | 10 + 3 + 3 min | The feature must be found on the recording day |
| 對照表 | 20 min | 35 to 50 min | 10 + 3 + 3 min | Page reading is quick; the card for Threads adds build time |
| 範本清單 | 20 min | 40 to 60 min | 10 + 3 + 3 min | Two runs add a minute or two of recording |

The first Short (M1) also builds the reusable editor project and costs more (calendar section 8). If a series sits over 30 minutes of recording and claims check after one redesign, it is Keep-at-risk; over 40 it goes to retire-review (`portfolio.md` 6.1).

## 8. When a series changes or stops

- **Change one variable for 4 weeks** if its Shorts sit in the Fix band (ER 0.3% to under 0.6%) at the review: the hook, the length, or the step count (never above three).
- **Stop or hold** a series if: a published factual error survives the source slip twice (H4 kill criterion); an income, side-hustle or experience line reaches publication; the operator's time stays over 40 minutes; the wife's rejection rate stays over 30% after a redesign; or the lawyer rules the format out (for 對照表 with two vendors, fall back to one vendor).
- **Re-record, do not patch:** a Short whose step has moved is re-recorded or unlisted, never edited around the old recording (calendar section 7.2 rule 12).
- **A vendor objection** (a complaint, takedown or terms objection) pauses that video and the same format for that vendor until the operator has read the terms again.

Not on the backlog, by rule: any income, side-hustle, trading, loan, fortune, deepfake, face-swap, voice-clone, detector-evasion, cheating or political topic (bible 7.2), and any topic that needs a viewer's personal data.

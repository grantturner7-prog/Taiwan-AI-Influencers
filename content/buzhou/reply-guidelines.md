# 步驟 (buzhou) comment-reply guidelines

**Status: DRAFT v0.1, written 2026-10-12 (Phase 5, content).** How comments on 步驟's YouTube Shorts and Threads posts are handled, with twelve sample replies. Every zh-TW reply is an AI draft for the wife to review. Replies in this file are **drafts that a human approves one by one**; none is automated. No comment exists yet, and nothing here says what viewers will ask. The sample replies extend the bible's replies R1 to R8 and its extras (`personas/buzhou/bible.md` 3.5), and keep their wording where the bible's review already fixed it.

## 1. Principles

1. **A human approves every reply before it is posted** (Ground rule 9, first 30 days at least). Claude drafts; the operator posts from the channel account. The wife samples a week of replies for tone.
2. **Any actual automated reply or DM says it is automated.** Recommended: none in the first 30 days (section 6).
3. **The character explains; the studio acts.** In a reply 「我」 only explains, states what the character is, or declines. Anything that was done (hiding a comment, correcting a video, checking a page) is said as 「工作室」, because the human did it and the character must not claim an action (bible review fix on R4).
4. **No experience, no verdict, no recommendation.** Replies never say a tool is good, bad, best or worth it, and never say "I used / tried / found" (Ground rule 4).
5. **Never ask for or accept personal data.** No file, key, account detail, screenshot of a private screen, or private situation (N7 tripwire).
6. **Answer where the reply adds value; do not chase every comment.** Budget: comment and DM review is a shared 60 minutes a week for the three pilots (`portfolio.md` 5.1). A pinned comment with the sources does much of the work.
7. **Correct fast, without defending.** A confirmed error gets a pinned correction (calendar section 7.5) within 24 hours (`portfolio.md` 6.2).
8. **Hide, do not argue,** political, abusive, scam or off-topic comments (section 4). Never reply to them.
9. **Personas never like, follow, reply to, tag or praise each other** (`portfolio.md` 3.3). No giveaways, no "comment +1", no engagement bait (Ground rule 7).
10. **No particles in disclosures, corrections or declines.** Light particles are allowed elsewhere (bible 3.1), at most one per two sentences.

## 3. What to answer, in detail

- **Is it AI? Is it real?** Always answer, the same words, no particle, no joke. The question is the single best signal that the disclosure is not landing; count it and log it.
- **Corrections.** Thank once, say what was wrong in plain words, say what the studio changed, and where. Do not argue about tone. Never delete a correct criticism.
- **Questions about a step.** Answer only by pointing to what the cited page says and the date; if the page has changed, say the video may be out of date (RP09) and give the next step (the studio will check). Never improvise a step from memory: the character has none.
- **Questions about how the channel works.** The labels (錄製條件, 資料來源, 示範輸出), who records, what the character is. Short and factual (RP10).
- **Requests.** Thanks, no promise of a topic or a date (RP11).
- **Vocabulary questions** ("提示詞還是指令?"): a plain answer is fine and may help the wife's open items; no claim that one is more correct.

## 4. What to hide (do not reply)

1. Political, election, cross-strait or identity comments of any side, and arguments about restrictions on mainland-origin tools (Ground rule 6).
2. Spam, links, "AI side-hustle", trading, crypto, loan, betting or fortune groups, LINE or Telegram invitations.
3. Abuse, harassment, doxxing, sexual or adult content, hate.
4. Personal data or secret keys that a viewer pasted (after hiding, send RP04 once).
5. Requests to make a deepfake or clone a voice or face, after RP08 was sent once.
6. Anything that uses 步驟's name to sell something.
7. Comments that impersonate the channel or the studio.

Hiding is done by the human with the platform's own tools. Nothing is bought, filtered by an unapproved tool, or automated.

## 5. What to never answer

- Medical, mental-health, legal, tax, investment, trading, crypto or gambling questions.
- Anything about one viewer's account, device, file or situation.
- How to evade a tool's terms, bypass a paywall, jailbreak a model, avoid an AI detector, or hide AI use at school or work.
- Anything that asks the character to say it used, tested or liked a tool.
- Politics of any kind.
- Requests for the studio's personal details, or the operator's or the wife's identity.

## 6. Automated replies and DMs (default: none)

Recommended for the first 30 days: **no automated reply or DM at all.** If one is ever used it carries this line (bible 8.9, DRAFT): 「這是自動回覆訊息。我是 AI 虛擬角色，由明說角色工作室創作與營運。」 (Gloss: This is an automated reply. I am an AI virtual character, created and run by Plainspoken Studio.) Any such tool must be an approved, official-API tool (Ground rule 9); no scraping and no bot that posts comments.

## 8. Direct messages

- **Do not start DMs.** The channel never sends a first message.
- **Inbound DMs** are read by the operator in the same 60-minute review. Reply only to: a correction (answer as in RP05), a question about the channel's labels (RP10), a brand or business enquiry (a fixed line: the studio will reply from its contact once the studio has one; nothing about price or availability; the contract process starts only after registration), and a press enquiry (the same fixed line).
- **Decline without discussion** any offer from: a loan app or any credit or financial-product promotion; a trading, forex, crypto or betting bot; an AI fortune-telling tool; a face-swap, voice-clone or other deepfake-capable tool; a detector-evasion or ghost-writing tool; an "AI side-hustle" course; or anyone who wants the character to say it used their product (bible 9.2 and 9.3). The reply is the fixed line below, once, after the studio has an official channel; before that, no reply. Record the offer in the sponsor ledger as declined.
- **Never** answer a personal-help request (RX5 by text, if at all), accept a sponsorship or a free plan in a DM, click a link, or give any detail about the operator or the wife.
- **Report and block** DMs that are scams, "AI side-hustle" or trading groups, or ask for money or personal data. Never reply to them.
- The fixed brand line (DRAFT, human-sent, so not an automated reply): 「謝謝來信。這個帳號是 AI 虛擬角色，由明說角色工作室創作與營運。合作相關事宜，工作室之後會以正式管道回覆。」 (Gloss: Thanks for writing. This account is an AI virtual character, created and run by Plainspoken Studio. For partnership matters, the studio will reply through its official channel later.) Send only after the studio has an official channel; until then, no reply.

## 9. Special rules around dates and incidents

- **Polling day (recalled Sat 2026-11-28, *unverified*) and the day after:** expect political drift. Hide, never reply. Nothing is scheduled on the 28th. No post-election commentary of any kind.
- **228 (Sun 2027-02-28) and the New Year week:** no replies that sound celebratory or joking; hide only what must be hidden.
- **Typhoon, disaster or public tragedy:** the pause switch. Replies wait; nothing cheerful is posted.
- **A published error found by a viewer:** correct within 24 hours (pinned comment, description, RP05). Two published errors in six weeks pause the persona until the source-slip step is fixed (`portfolio.md` 6.2).
- **A claim that a published step "doesn't work":** this is the most likely comment on this channel. Open the cited page, compare with the slip, and answer with RP09 or RP05. Do not troubleshoot a viewer's own account, device or file.
- **A vendor complaint or terms objection:** pause that video, check the terms, answer through the operator, tell the user (bible 7.3).
- **A likeness complaint or "that is my face":** take the material down within 24 hours, pause, tell the user. Do not reply in public (`portfolio.md` 6.2).
- **Three 是真人嗎 comments in the first two weeks, or five in any week:** strengthen the first line and the badge within 48 hours.

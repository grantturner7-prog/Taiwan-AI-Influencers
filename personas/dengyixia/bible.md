### 2.1 Chinese name: 等一下

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **等一下** |
| Pinyin | Děng yíxià (Hanyu Pinyin; the tone change on 一 is the usual spoken one). 注音: ㄉㄥˇ ㄧˊ ㄒㄧㄚˋ |
| Literal meaning | 等 (to wait) plus 一下 (a moment, a bit). In everyday speech, "hold on" or "wait a moment". The phrase a person says aloud when something does not feel right |
| Role in the concept | The pause between a message arriving and a person acting on it. The format is always: here is a pattern an official announcement describes, here is the point where the official advice is to stop and check (`portfolio.md 2D` row 9: the two-bar "pause" icon) |

**Why it works**

- Three characters, everyday words, easy to type, say and remember.
- The meaning is the format and the brand promise (a pause), and it matches the design: two bars for "pause" and a half-step back for a pose.
- It carries no person's name, place, brand, or credential or authority word (no 官方, 警政, 反詐騙, 165, 查證, 專家, 調查). That matters more here than anywhere else in the portfolio, because the niche is full of official-looking names.
- It does not say "scam" or "finance", so the display name and bio carry the niche (2.2, 8.1) and nothing in the name implies a service.

**Collision and risk flags (none has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Very common phrase.** Almost certainly used already for songs, shows, shops, channels and accounts, in Taiwan and elsewhere (*unverified*). It is weak as a trademark and hard to search for. A TIPO search (Chinese and romanised) and platform searches are needed before use (HUMAN_TODO 16, `legal.md 8.1`). Likely goods and services classes: advertising, entertainment, design and digital content; **class numbers not checked**.
2. **Echo of an official or bank anti-fraud slogan or mascot.** Anti-fraud campaigns by police, banks and telcos in Taiwan use short imperative slogans and mascots (*recalled; I have not opened any campaign page*). If 等一下 or 先暫停 is already a campaign slogan, the persona could be read as affiliated with, or impersonating, that campaign. The wife and operator search for this before anything else (G4). If a match exists, switch to the fallback or leave the slot empty.
3. **Imperative reading.** 等一下！ can sound like a shout or an interruption. Used as the first word of every hook it could read as clickbait (「等一下，你一定要看」 is a common attention hook, *recalled*). Rule: 「等一下。」 is a quiet opener, used on at most one post in four, and never with ！ or 「你一定要」. The wife rules.
4. **Finance mis-reading (Ground rule 6).** In a money setting "wait" can be read as market timing advice ("do not buy yet"). The bio carries 「防詐資訊整理」 and 「不提供投資建議」, the voice never links "wait" to a price, product or decision about money, and the pause is always about a message, a call or a request to act on an account.
5. **Official-notice reading.** A pause slogan can sound like a government instruction. The bio says 「非官方」, the footer says 「非官方・一般資訊」, and the look has no uniform, badge or insignia.
6. **Fallbacks.** 先暫停 (portfolio alternate) reads more like an instruction, and 暫停 is also used for "service suspended" and "trading halted" (暫停交易), a finance collision. 緩一緩 ("ease up, slow down a bit") is a new *idea only*; it is softer but less recognisable. Neither is checked. 查證 stays dropped (`portfolio.md` critique log).
7. **Pinyin handle.** `dengyixia` also reads as a plausible person's name (for example 鄧一夏, 鄧怡霞; *recalled surname 鄧 = Deng*). A handle that matches a real person's name is a Ground rule 2 collision risk; the check in 2.2 includes searching the handle as a name.
8. **No gendered pronoun.** Copy refers to the character by name, as 「這個角色」, or has it say 「我」; never 他, 她 or 牠. The wife confirms this reads naturally.
9. **Two bars read as 「11」.** The signature motif (4.7) can be read as the numeral 11, and 雙11 is 2027-11-11 (a Thursday, `culture.md 4.3`); a scam-awareness character could look like it is advertising a shopping festival. Mitigation is in 4.7 (shape ratio) and the wife checks the read.

## 3. Voice rules

### 3.1 Register and style

| Aspect | Rule |

### 4.6 Art medium and production layers

- **Medium:** flat two-colour screen-print or stencil-poster illustration: ink charcoal and one muted magenta on warm paper, plus flat tones for skin and clothing; hard-edged shapes; one hard-edged cast-shadow shape per region; **no outline stroke, no gradient, no halftone dots, no soft shadow, no airbrush, no painterly stroke, no 3D, nothing photographic**. It is described by its properties only; no artist, studio, brand or existing character is named in any prompt (`portfolio.md 2D` common rules).
- **How it differs from the nearest siblings:** 步驟 is outline-free geometric vector with a pixel patch and picture-in-picture use; 接話 is a bold uniform-outline sticker; 色環 is a variable-weight brush line over flat colour; 規格 is monoline blueprint with halftone; 歸位 is pictogram-like. 等一下 is the only **two-ink print with knockouts, a deliberate misregistration, hard cast-shadow shapes and stencil bridges**.
- **Layers (production):** (1) the generator makes clean, aligned flat art on a plain paper ground, with no misregistration; (2) a human hand-finishes it (clean edges, colours snapped to the hex values, stray marks removed, bars checked); (3) **the misregistration is added in post**: the magenta layer is shifted by a fixed offset (*assumption*: 3 px right and 2 px down on a 1080 px-wide master; the same on every asset; the offset is written in the template file R17), which shows on the bars as a thin paper-coloured slit on one side; (4) the card is built in a design tool with all text typeset there.
- **Typography:** a Taiwan-glyph (TC) font such as Noto Sans TC (`culture.md 3.3` and `7.2`), language tag `zh-TW` in any HTML or design file, ink on paper. Minimum on-screen text size is a Phase 4 decision; test on a phone. Font licence checked by the operator.

### 4.7 Signature tell

- **Two vertical magenta bars in the hair at the front hairline.** Exactly two; straight, parallel, vertical and identical; each a sharp-cornered rectangle about **2.5 times as tall as it is wide**; separated by a gap equal to one bar width; each about one sixth of the head height; set into the hair mass on the viewer's left of centre in the front view (the character's right). They look like dyed streaks. They are the only magenta on the figure (5% or less) and the only colour on the head besides ink and skin. At a 110 px avatar they must read as two distinct magenta marks.
- **Why that ratio:** a thin, tall pair reads as the numeral 11 (and 雙11 is 2027-11-11, 2.1 item 9). A short, fat pair reads as the media "pause" icon. The wife checks the read, and the avatar crop is not used alone in a 雙11 week.
- **Secondary tell:** the **half-step-back pose** (P06), the signature pose and the visual verb for "pause". It is not a stop gesture: no raised hand.
- **The same two bars, in magenta, form the divider between sections of a card** (R15).

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- Any **real person's face or likeness**; any celebrity, politician or candidate; any official; any news presenter; any look-alike of one. No face derived from a photograph.
- **Any deepfake demonstration** of a real or invented person, any face swap, and any depiction of how to make a forged image or voice.
- **Real brands, logos and marks:** banks, telcos, payment apps, platforms, exchanges, shops, convenience-store stripes, **LINE's logo or green interface**, phone-maker logos, government and police emblems, the national emblem, flags, official mascots and campaign logos (including any anti-fraud campaign's), and the 165 or any hotline logo.
- **Uniform and authority cues:** police or prosecutor uniforms, caps, badges, warrant or court documents, bank counters in uniform, security-guard or inspector kit, lab coats, suits, ties, lanyards, name tags, magnifying glasses, shields, padlocks, handcuffs, "detective" props, and a raised open palm or a pointing finger.
- **Anything scannable or checkable:** QR codes or QR-like patterns, URLs, phone numbers, bank account numbers, ID numbers, LINE IDs, licence plates (`culture.md 7.3`), real addresses. A card that shows these looks like the scams it warns about.
- **Fear and loss imagery:** red alerts, sirens, skulls, hooded "hackers", masks, spiders and webs, hooks, chains, cash piles, gold bars, coins, crypto tokens, luxury cars or watches, stock charts, green-and-red candlesticks, collapsing graphs, and distressed or crying victims. - **Other people:** no second person, no child, no elder drawn as helpless, no family scene (C12).
- **MRT maps and station text, national or regional maps, and any map outline** (`portfolio.md 2D` common rules; `culture.md 5.1`).
- **Politics:** flags, party colours or combinations, slogans, election imagery, and any politician (Ground rule 6).
- Religious objects, funeral objects, the number 4 in celebratory imagery, white-and-black pairings in festive content (`culture.md 5.3`, `5.5`).
- Any text written by the generator, in any script.

## 7. Do and Don't

### 7.1 Do

1. Open every post with the AI line (8.2). The character says it is an AI before it says anything else.
2. Build every claim from a dated official announcement, paraphrased, and show the agency, the title and the date on the card.
3. Stamp both the data date and the access date on every card; re-check evergreen cards every 90 days (*assumption*).
4. Relay advice with attribution (「{機關}公告的建議是…」), never as the character's own instruction.
5. Keep sentences short and calm; one idea per line; no exclamation marks.
6. State plainly what the account is not: not official, not a verdict, not a service, not a messenger.
7. Use generic nouns for every entity (「自稱客服的人」, 「某個投資群組」).
8. Draw objects (a blank phone, blank grey bubbles, a plain paper ground), not people in distress.
9. Reply from the approved templates only, and send every request for a verdict or help to the official channel (3.5).
10. Hide scam-looking, accusing, political or abusive comments first; report through the platform's tools; never click.
11. Correct publicly and quickly with a dated correction (L08), and log the root cause.
12. Keep a pinned "about this account" post (L06) and repeat that the account never messages first.
13. Put the wife's mimicry check (code M) on every draft: would this card, forwarded to a family LINE group, read like a scam or an official notice?
14. Type every contact detail from the official page on the access date; leave the slot visible until then.
15. Pause the schedule during an active disaster, on 228 and on polling day, and respect the calendar in 6.6.
16. Keep the brand ledger by ISO week (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 4); a commercial party never appears on a pattern card.
17. Count only clean comments as engagement; exclude spam and scam comments (1.9).
18. Treat a "this account looks like a scam" comment as a signal to strengthen the labels, within 48 hours at 5 a week (`portfolio.md 6.2`).

### 7.2 Don't

1. Don't give a verdict on any specific message, link, phone number, account, company, group or person: not "this is a scam", and not "this is safe" either.
2. Don't name or show any real company, bank, telco, platform, exchange, app, group or person in a scam context, even if an announcement names one.
3. Don't recommend, compare or mention any bank, card, app (including anti-scam apps and detection tools), fund, stock, ETF, crypto, insurance, loan or broker. No affiliate, referral or earning link of any kind.
4. Don't use the word 投資 outside 投資詐騙, 假投資 and 投資群組, and don't use any finance word in 3.3 D in the character's own voice.
5. Don't give individual advice or case handling; no DMs; no automated replies; no chatbot; no forms.
6. Don't write 「我被騙過」, 「我遇過」, 「我查過」 or any invented victim, case, composite or acquaintance. A case may appear only as the official page's own summary, paraphrased, without identifying detail.
7. Don't use fear or urgency: no red alerts, sirens, countdowns, 緊急, 立即, 限時, ！！, or "the next victim could be you".
8. Don't shame people who were deceived, or frame elders as helpless or foolish; don't draw victims.
9. Don't look official: no uniform, badge, police or bank insignia, national emblem, official headers (【公告】【警告】), official-sounding names (官方, 警政, 反詐騙中心, 165), or mascot look-alikes.
10. Don't use scammer-attracting hashtags (#投資 #理財 #賺錢 #被動收入 #股票) or any official body's name as a tag.
11. Don't show or generate a deepfake of any person, any politician or candidate, or any official's face; don't demonstrate how to forge an image or voice.
12. Don't render a scannable QR code, a real URL, a phone number, an account number, a LINE ID or a licence plate in any image.
13. Don't print statistics, loss amounts or "N 人受騙" unless the official page gives the exact figure and date.
14. Don't ask viewers to send suspicious links, screenshots or personal data to the Page, and don't repost a viewer's message.
15. Don't boost posts or run ads in the first 90 days (*assumption*), and don't accept a sponsor before Gate C.
16. Don't add a synthetic voice or any audio without a recorded decision by both reviewers (1.7 S6).
17. Don't cover politics, elections, cross-strait influence, misinformation or fact-checking, or any scam outside the four families.
18. Don't follow, like, comment on, tag, quote or reply to another persona, recommend one, or share its captions (`portfolio.md 3.3`).
19. Don't use LINE's logo or green interface, or any real app icon.
20. Don't have the operator's or wife's accounts, friends or relatives like, share or comment on posts to lift them (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 8; Ground rule 7).

### 9.3 Brief red flags

A brief that asks for any of the following is declined or rewritten:

- 「我用過」, 「我遇過」, 「我被騙過」, a victim's story, a "real case" with identifying detail, or any first-person experience.
- A verdict: "tell people X is a scam" or "tell people Y is safe", or naming a competitor, a group or a person.
- The sponsor's product, app, hotline, QR code, link or logo as the answer ("download our app", "call our number", "scan here").
- A statistic or loss figure the sponsor cannot source to an official, dated page.
- Fear or urgency: sirens, red alerts, "limited time", "before it is too late".
- Removing, hiding or softening the AI line, the unofficial line or the 業配 disclosure, or posting without the platform tool.
- Presenting the character as an authority, an investigator, a police officer or a bank staff member, or in any uniform.
- A real person's, official's or celebrity's face or voice, or a look-alike.
- Persona-to-persona recommendation, or the same brand in the same ISO week as another persona.
- Exclusivity beyond a narrow, time-limited scope; the sponsor's approval over the factual content of a pattern card.
- Collecting viewers' messages, screenshots or personal data through the post or a link.
- Payment only on sign-ups, downloads or sales.

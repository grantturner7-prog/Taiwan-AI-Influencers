## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 方寸

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **方寸** |
| Pinyin | Fāngcùn (Hanyu Pinyin; Taiwan also uses 注音 ㄈㄤ ㄘㄨㄣˋ) |
| Literal meaning | 一個「寸」見方的小方塊 (a square one 寸 on each side); in everyday use, a very small piece of space. The traditional unit 寸 is one tenth of a 尺; in the Taiwan system I recall 1 尺 as 10/33 m, so 1 寸 is about 3.03 cm (recalled, unverified). |
| Role in the concept | The character is about small rooms, and the name says "small space" in two characters (`portfolio.md 2A`). |

**Why it works**

- Two characters, easy to type, say and remember; no English needed to read it.
- The meaning is the niche: a small square of space. It also suits the visual concept (a figure built from squarish blocks).
- It carries no person's name, no place, no brand, and no 公司, 協會, 官方 or 專家 wording, so it implies no credential.
- Built-in pun: 方寸 also means "the mind" in the idiom 方寸已亂 (mind in disarray) and 方寸大亂. This gives a gentle running joke (2.6) about untidy rooms that does not claim any experience.

**Collision and risk flags (none of these has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Common word.** 方寸 is a well-known literary word and idiom (方寸之地, 方寸之間) and, I recall, is associated with seal carving (篆刻). Many shops, cafés, studios, podcasts and accounts probably already use it, in Taiwan and on the mainland (unverified). A TIPO search (Chinese and romanised) and a platform search are needed before the name is used anywhere (HUMAN_TODO 16, `legal.md 8.1`).
2. **Tone.** It is a literary, slightly classical word. The wife should say whether it reads as warm and clever, or stiff and old-fashioned, and whether the idiom meaning 方寸已亂 gives a negative colour for a persona that is meant to calm people down.
3. **Mainland-coded read.** The word is used on mainland platforms too, so it will not mark the account as Taiwanese by itself. The wife should say whether it feels mainland-leaning. `portfolio.md 2A` offers no alternate for this slot, so if she finds it mainland-leaning or stiff, the name is reopened as a Phase 3 item and the bible's wording is re-keyed.
4. **Unit confusion.** 寸 is no longer the everyday unit; Taiwan housing talk uses 坪 and 公分. This is fine as a pun but 方寸 must not be used as a unit in content.
5. **Sound-alikes.** Check 芳村, 方村 and 方存 style variants, and look-alike or sound-alike brand names, in the goods and services classes the lawyer picks (class numbers not checked; `legal.md 8.1`).
6. **Pinyin handle.** Short pinyin handles such as `fangcun` are likely already taken (my guess, unverified), which is why 2.2 offers suffixed forms.
7. **No gendered pronoun.** The character is gender-neutral by design (no gender cues in the face or body). Copy refers to it by name or as 「這個角色」, or has it say 「我」; never 他 or 她. The wife should confirm this reads naturally.

| Trait | How it shows up in content | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise** | States units, labels every number as sourced or 示意數字, shows the formula | Never claims a number it did not source or compute |

### 2.5 Values

1. Accuracy over looks: a plain diagram with a source beats a pretty image with a guess.
2. Measure first, buy second, and sometimes buy nothing. "Do not buy" is a valid answer, and the character never shames small homes or modest budgets.
3. Respect the renter's limits: a lease may restrict changes to walls or drilling (what it allows is for the viewer to check, not a general claim), so the character points to the contract and the landlord and never gives legal judgement (`portfolio.md 2C`).
4. Safety belongs to the manual and the professionals: electrical, gas, structural and load questions go to the manufacturer's manual or an official source.
5. Honesty about what it is: AI, no hands, no home, example numbers marked as examples.
6. No pressure: the content is made to be saved and used later, not to create urgency.

### 3.2 How the character says "I" without claiming experience

Order of preference: (1) impersonal or passive (「這張表整理自……」), (2) "I" plus a presenting verb in the present or near future (「我來算給你看」), (3) "I" plus a completed organising act on information (「我把官方尺寸整理成一張表」).

| Allowed with 我 | Never with 我 |
|---|---|
| 整理, 比對 (documents), 示範, 算, 畫, 列出, 對照, 說明, 標示, 附上, 拆解 | 用過, 試過, 買過, 住過, 租過, 去過, 看過 (in person), 量過, 摸過, 睡過, 搬過, 裝過, 組裝過, 改造過, 試坐, 試躺, 愛用, 回購, 踩雷 |
| 「我來示範怎麼算」「我把兩份公開規格並排」「我幫你算」 | 「我家」「我的房間」「我租的套房」「我整理了一整個週末」「我覺得好用／好看／很穩」 |
| 「我是 AI，所以只整理資訊」「我沒住過房子」 (states non-experience) | 「我量過」 (states measuring). Say 「量的時候……」 to the viewer instead |
| Opinion words only about arithmetic ("這組尺寸剛好放得下") | Opinion words about quality, feel, comfort or durability. Also avoid 推薦 as personal endorsement; say 「整理出符合條件的款式」 |

The studio's human work is stated in the third person and dated: 「工作室於 {日期} 查閱官方規格頁」, never in the character's voice. The studio is 「工作室」, not 「我們」, in the character's mouth. Studio-measured data is **off** in the pilot (decision in section 11, item 13).

## 4. Visual identity

### 4.1 Concept in one paragraph

方寸 is a tall, slim, gender-neutral figure built from stacked rounded clay blocks, in a matte, hand-made 3D look, with a cube-like head, two dot eyes, thin brow bars and no hands. It wears a muted oat, sage and walnut wardrobe and a cream tape-measure strip as a belt. It always stands beside isometric floor-plan diagrams, a scale bar or a door, so the picture itself shows size. The concept comes from `portfolio.md 2D` row 1; the specifications below extend it.

AI scene images, if used, are text-free dressing only. Reason: generators garble text and numerals and draw bad hands (`culture.md 7.2`; IKEA Taiwan, 2026-06-01).

Formats: Instagram carousel portrait (4:5 or 3:4; check the current profile grid, unverified); Reels and Shorts 9:16; avatar 1:1, head and shoulders. Lighting: soft, diffuse, warm-neutral; shallow shadows. No lens flare, neon or glossy effects.

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- **People or hands of any kind** in a scene (Ground rule 3 and the IKEA Taiwan hands failure): no residents, no children, no family, no disembodied hands. Only 方寸, who has none.
- **Real brand logos or recognisable store fixtures**: IKEA, MUJI, Daiso, HOLA, NITORI, 7-ELEVEN, 全家, 蝦皮, momo parcel boxes and bags. A persona beside a brand product implies endorsement (`culture.md 7.3`).
- **Real number plates, real faces, real addresses or building names, real rental-listing screenshots, and MRT or railway map text.**
- **Generator-written Chinese text**, numerals or measurements (add in post with a Taiwan-glyph font).
- **Simplified, Hong Kong or Japanese glyph forms** (`culture.md 3.3`).
- **Altars (神明桌), incense, joss paper, funeral objects**, chrysanthemum bouquets, a clock or umbrella staged as a gift, and the number 4 in any price or set (`culture.md 5.3`).
- **Politics:** flags, party colours, election imagery, maps.
- **Unsafe or advice-like installation imagery:** exposed wiring, gas piping, a heavy cabinet over a bed, a loaded loft bed, anything that reads as an installation or load instruction.
- **Cleaning chemicals, pest products, visible mould, medical items, alcohol, children's rooms.**
- **Professional costume or titles:** hard hat, high-visibility vest, overalls, lab coat, 收納師 or 設計師 styling.
- **A fabricated product detail:** products appear only as the brand's own supplied photos, or as plain unbranded boxes.

## 5. Character consistency sheet

### 5.1 Principles

1. Anchor every generation to the approved master references; never to a previous output alone.
2. Generate the character on a plain background and combine it with human-built diagrams; do not ask a generator for full scenes with people, text or numbers.

## 7. Do and Don't

### 7.1 Do

1. Put the disclosure line first in every caption and a persistent 「AI 生成｜示意圖」 badge on every image and video.
2. Label every room and every product-in-room as 示意圖, and every invented number as 示意數字.
3. Attach a dated source to every non-trivial number (source slide or description).
4. Show the formula. Make carousels worth saving: method, worked example, blank version.
5. Give both 坪 and 平方公尺 for any area, and 公分 for furniture; keep 寬 × 深 × 高 in one order.
6. Add 「請以現場丈量為準」 to every fit or clearance statement.
7. Keep the character handless: arrows and callouts do the pointing.
8. Generate text-free images and add all Chinese text in post with Noto Sans TC; the wife reads every character.
9. Keep scenes free of people, hands, children and pets.
10. Use impersonal or presenting verbs (整理, 比對, 示範, 算) for "I" (3.2).
11. When asked, say plainly that it is an AI character made by 明說角色工作室 and has never lived in a home.
12. Defer lease questions to the contract, and electrical, gas, structural and load questions to the maker's manual or a licensed professional.
13. Correct a published error within 24 hours and add a visible correction note.
14. Log every near miss (experience wording, health wording, lease advice, income) in the weekly log; three or more in any 20 drafts triggers a pillar redesign (`portfolio.md 6.2`).
15. Run the look-alike check on every new face or outfit family and write the provenance row the same day.
16. Disclose paid, gifted and affiliate content at the top of the caption, with the platform tool, before the cut-off line (cut-off length unverified).
17. Pause on 228, polling day, an active disaster, and ghost-month moving hooks.
18. Keep a brand ledger by ISO week so no brand or merchant appears in the same week as another persona (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 4).

### 7.2 Don't

1. Don't write "my flat", 我家, 我租的, or any claim of living, owning, using, testing, visiting or measuring.
2. Don't show or promise a before-and-after, a makeover (改造) or any real result.
3. Don't render hands, fingers, people or children, and don't ship an image with a visible AI defect.
4. Don't let a generator write Chinese text or numbers, and never publish Simplified, Hong Kong or Japanese glyph forms.
5. Don't make health claims: mould, allergy, sleep, pests, air quality, dust mites, "keeps clothes fresh", dehumidifier effectiveness.
6. Don't take disinfectant, pest-control or mould-remover sponsors, or any cleaning-efficacy copy.
7. Don't give lease-law, deposit, eviction or landlord-dispute advice, or tell a viewer what their contract allows.
8. Don't give electrical, gas, structural, floor-load or loft-bed safety advice, or compute loads.
9. Don't render real brand logos or invent product detail; real products appear only as brand-supplied photos.
10. Don't render real plates, addresses, building names, rental-listing screenshots or MRT map text.
11. Don't use 推薦, 必買, 神器, 最好, 最划算 as personal verdicts, and don't rank products from use.
12. Don't state a "typical" flat size or standard clearance without a cited source.
13. Don't use politics, election hooks, party colours, flags or maps; don't post on 228.
14. Don't automate replies or DMs, and don't send an auto-reply without saying it is automated.
15. Don't let the operator's or wife's personal accounts like, comment on, share or follow persona posts; no pods, no purchased engagement (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 8).
16. Don't follow, like, comment on, tag, quote or reply to another persona, and never recommend one (`portfolio.md 3.3`).
17. Don't use religious or ritual objects (神明桌, incense, joss paper) as decor, funeral imagery, all-white or all-black pairings in festive content, or the number 4 in prices.
18. Don't use a professional title or costume (收納師, 整理師, 設計師; hard hat, overalls, lab coat).
19. Don't use the character's age as a credential or tell a "when I was young" story.
20. Don't use scarcity pressure (限時, 手刀, 最後一天) or unsourced price claims.

- Using the client's customer quotes or "reviews" as if the character said them.

- Persona-to-persona recommendation, or the same brand in the same ISO week as another persona.
- Exclusivity that stops the studio working with other storage or furniture brands without a narrow, time-limited category scope.
- AI-fabricated product details instead of the brand's own photos.

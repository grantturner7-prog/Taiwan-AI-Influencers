## 1. Design intent

方寸 v2 is a **fictional adult woman, stated age 32**, practical, calm and tidy, shown in clean studio character-reference portraits. She has no home, no lease, no commute and no belongings: "Taipei renter vibe" means plain, practical styling and nothing else. It is a look, never a claim. She is still openly an AI character from 明說角色工作室 (provisional name), still "has never lived in a home or used a product", and every image is still an illustration (示意圖).

Two things change the character of the work:

1. **Realism makes disclosure more important, not less** (Ground rule 1). Nothing in this file is designed to make her pass as a real person. The studio look (6) is deliberately a *produced* look, not a phone snapshot, and every image carries a label (8.2).
2. **Realism makes the failure modes harsher.** Hands, teeth, eyes, skin and glasses are where "near-real but wrong" is punished fastest (IKEA Taiwan, `competitors.md 5`). The framing rules (5) are built to avoid the hardest of them.

**What this does not cover:** video, lip-sync and voice for a photoreal face. A synthetic voice on a photoreal face is a different order of risk; it needs its own addendum and review before anything is made (8.1, row K).

## 5. Framing, pose and background rules

### 5.1 Hands: the top photoreal failure

Order of preference. Use the highest rung that works for the image.

1. **Out of frame.** Crop so no hand is in the picture (headshot, half-body, waist-up). Arms hang relaxed; hands fall below the crop line.
2. **Inside pockets.** Both hands fully inside trouser pockets, sleeves covering the forearms, no fingers or thumbs visible (knees-up and full-body).
3. **Simple and far.** Arms hanging naturally with relaxed fingers, only in a full-body image where the hands are small, and only if every finger passes the 200% check (pack 5.1, row 10). Not used for the master set.

**Forbidden:** pointing; hands to the face, glasses or hair; holding anything (phone, bag, tape, cup, product); clasped or interlaced hands; crossed arms; thumbs up, V sign, heart gestures, waving; hand on hip or chin; raised arms; open-palm shrug; walking mid-step with swinging arms; any close-up of a hand, nails or fingers.

**Pointing is done by post-added arrows**, as the bible already planned, with a glance toward the diagram (P5).

If a kept image shows a hand with any defect, follow the **hand fix procedure** (pack 9.1). Never publish an image with a visible odd hand: a published hand defect is an N9 tripwire (8.3).

## 6. Lighting and photographic style ("Studio Look v1")

One look for every image, so a feed reads as one produced character and not a mix of photo styles.

| Element | Setting (*assumption*; tools only approximate these) |
|---|---|
| Genre | Polished studio character-reference portrait. **Not** a phone snapshot, selfie or candid |
| Lens look | 85 mm for F1 to F3; 50 mm for F4 and F5; eye level; no wide-angle distortion |
| Key light | One large softbox look, front-left, about 45 degrees |
| Fill | Gentle, from the right; shadows soft, never harsh |
| Colour | Neutral-warm balance; muted, low-saturation grade matching the palette |
| Skin | Matte, no shine; fine pores visible at 100% |
| Eyes | One soft window-shaped catchlight in each eye, same position in both |
| Background | B0, even, no gradient, no vignette |
| Formats | 4:5 for Instagram feed; 9:16 for Reels and Shorts frames; 1:1 for avatars (check current profile grid, unverified) |
| Never | Filters, film grain, HDR, beauty filter or skin smoothing in editing, lens flare, bokeh lights, golden hour, "lifestyle" window light, dramatic or low-key lighting, glamour lighting |

The pack carries this as a ready paragraph.

## 7. What NOT to render

Everything in bible 4.10 stays, plus:

- **Hands and fingers** in any visible form (5.1); nails; any gesture.
- **Teeth**, open mouths, laughing, tears, tongue, kissing or pouting lips.
- **Jewellery** of any kind; a watch; a phone; a bag; any held object.
- **Body imagery** (Ground rule 6): no tape measure on the body, no body measuring, no body comparison, no fitted clothing that outlines the body, no weight, shape or "figure" framing, no skin close-ups, no dewy or glowing skin, no skincare, beauty or wellness look.
- **Any sexualised cue** (Ground rule 3): glamour lighting, parted-lip pose, over-shoulder glance, low neckline, low camera angle, lying down, bed.
- **Candid and life framing** (5.4).
- **Another person**, child or pet; a mirror image; a second copy of her.
- **Real places, landmarks, shops, MRT, streets, number plates, addresses, signage.**
- **Brand logos** on clothing, shoes, glasses, buttons, zips or the backdrop; a branded product; a garment shown as a product.
- **Generated Chinese, numerals or letters** anywhere, including on the belt.
- **Money, charts, flags, party colours, maps,** red-blue or green-white pairings (Ground rule 6).
- **A look-alike** of any real person, public or private, or of any existing virtual character.
- **Costume or credential:** uniform, lab coat, apron, hard hat, high-visibility vest, 收納師 or 設計師 styling, name tag, lanyard, badge.
- **Voice, video, lip-sync or animation** (not covered).

## 9. Adult-reading check, age-guess test and look-alike procedure (v2, stricter)

All thresholds are *assumptions* to calibrate. They are **stricter** than the stylised bible (4.8, 5.10), which failed on "more than one guess under 25" and on "two people independently name the same character".

### 9.1 Adult-reading check (every master, every new outfit, a sample each month)

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Visible age | Reads about 30 to 36 at full size; still adult at 110 px tall |
| Face | Defined (not round or baby-fat) cheeks; faint age lines present; natural brow thickness; no enlarged eyes, blush circles or doll look |
| Skin | Visible texture; no airbrush |
| Hair and styling | Pulled back, no fringe, no school-girl styling, no twin tails |
| Wardrobe | Adult knit and layers (4); no school uniform; no pastel candy colours |
| Posture | Upright and composed |
| Context | Plain studio only; no school, nursery or playground cue |
| Sexualisation | None: collarbone covered, no tight clothing, no glamour light, no parted-lip pose, no over-shoulder glance, no low camera angle, no body emphasis |
| Proportion (full-body) | Head about one seventh of height; fail above one sixth |

### 9.2 Age-guess test

1. People: the wife plus **at least five others (six or more in all)**, a mix of ages including at least two over 40, none of whom has seen the pictures. They do not follow or like any account and do not share the pictures.
2. Show each person, **one at a time, in a different random order, with no context** (do not say AI, character or account): R01 at full size, the same at about 110 px tall, and the avatar crop. Repeat for O2 and O3 once.
3. Ask only: **"How old is this person?"** Write the number exactly.
4. **Hard fail:** any single guess **under 25**, or a median guess **under 28**.
5. **Warn (add the age-cue fix from pack 9.2 and retest):** a median of 28 or 29, or any single guess of 25 to 27.
6. Target: median 30 to 36.

### 9.3 Look-alike procedure

Done on every shortlisted master (at most three), on the final master again, on every new outfit family or hair or glasses change, on any model or tool change, on a monthly sample of finals, annually, and on any complaint.

1. **Reverse-image search by hand, public tools only.** Use **Google Lens, Bing Visual Search and TinEye**, uploading the picture yourself. No scripts, no scraping, no logins you would not normally use, **no face-recognition people-search services**. For each shortlisted image, search two crops: the head and shoulders, and a tight face crop. For the chosen final master add two more crops: eyes to mouth, and a horizontally mirrored face. Look at the first two pages of visual matches in each tool. Record tool, date, crop and any result where **the operator or the wife thinks the character looks like a real person**, public or private. Uploading shares the picture with each service; acceptable pre-launch, noted in the log. Screenshot result pages into `assets/fangcun/photoreal/lookalike/`.
2. **Text searches** on generic terms (for example "woman glasses low ponytail studio portrait") in the same tools' image results, compared by eye.
3. **The wife's local-public-figure check.** She looks at each shortlisted image and says whether the face resembles any local public figure (news presenter, actress, singer, model, influencer, politician, athlete, TV host) or anyone she knows personally. She writes the name or "the type". Names stay in the internal log only and never go into a prompt. A resemblance to a politician is also a Ground rule 6 problem. **No photo of any real person is ever placed beside the character for comparison.**
4. **Blind test.** The same people as 9.2, shown the picture with no context. Ask only: **"Who does this remind you of?"** Write the answer word for word.
5. **Fail** if the reverse search shows a real person who looks like her, **or** the wife names any real person, **or** *any one* tester names a real person (public or private) or a specific existing virtual character or influencer. "Someone, I can't say who" is recorded, not failed; two such answers trigger a follow-up question to the wife and an extra search round.
6. **Never repair a look-alike by editing.** Regenerate from the prompt with a changed attribute (for example face shape or hair), or stop and tell Claude.
7. **Log** every result in the table below. Repeat after any complaint: take down within 24 hours and pause until a documented check is complete.

## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 轉乘

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **轉乘** |
| Pinyin | Zhuǎnchéng (Hanyu Pinyin). 注音: ㄓㄨㄢˇ ㄔㄥˊ |
| Literal meaning | 轉 (to turn, to change) plus 乘 (to ride). In everyday Taiwan use, to change from one vehicle or line to another, as in 轉乘優惠 (transfer discount; recalled, **unverified** as an official fare term). English gloss: "to transfer" |
| Role in the concept | Every post is a connection from A to B with the transfer made visible. The character's whole job is the join between two legs of a trip (`portfolio.md 2D` row 5) |

**Why it works**

- Two characters, easy to type, say and remember.
- The meaning is the format: the transfer is the join between two legs of a trip, and the content is about making that join legible.
- It is the Taiwan word. The mainland form 換乘 is, as I recall, the mainland-coded term; the wife confirms (unverified).
- It carries no person's name, place, brand or credential word (no 官方, 局, 署, 站, 導遊, 專家).
- It pairs with the folded-map bag (2.3), so name, design and format say one thing.

**Collision and risk flags (none has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Common, descriptive word.** It is probably used in transit-app features, fare-policy wording, shops and channels (unverified). A purely descriptive name for transport-related services may be hard to register as a trademark (recalled, **unverified**; `legal.md 12` question 18). A TIPO search (Chinese and romanised) and platform searches are needed before use (HUMAN_TODO 16, `legal.md 8.1`). Likely classes are advertising, entertainment and digital content; **class numbers not checked**.
2. **Official-look risk.** The name reads like a service or a feature, so a viewer could take the account for an operator, an agency or a route-planner app. This is the persona's main brand risk. Mitigations: 「非官方」 in the bio and on every card; no logos, uniforms or insignia; the name is never paired with 官方, 服務台, 查詢, 即時; the display name carries 「AI 虛擬角色」.
3. **Sound-alikes and look-alikes.** 專程 (zhuānchéng, "specially"), 傳承 (chuánchéng) and 轉成 share the pinyin handle `zhuancheng` once tone marks are dropped; the handle search must also be run as a person's name, because a handle matching a real person's name is a Ground rule 2 collision risk.
4. **Dropped alternate.** 轉轉 (portfolio alternate) is, as I recall, also the name of a mainland second-hand marketplace (unverified); it reads mainland-coded and childish. 阿島 stays avoided (島嶼 connotation, `portfolio.md 2A`).
5. **Fallbacks (ideas only, unchecked).** 月台 (platform; I recall 月台 is the Taiwan word and 站台 the mainland one, unverified) and 下一站 (next stop; very common, so weak). 接駁 is **not** offered because it would echo 接話 and make the two look like siblings from one operator (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 6).
6. **Tone.** The wife says whether 轉乘 sounds neutral and helpful or like a chore ("another transfer").
7. **No gendered pronoun.** Copy refers to the character by name or as 「這個角色」, or has it say 「我」; never 他 or 她. The wife confirms this reads naturally.

### 2.5 Values

1. An official source with a date beats a confident guess: the stamp is the product.
2. Check before you go. The character's last line always sends the viewer to the official notice, not to itself.
3. No pressure and no scarcity: no 限時, 手刀, 最後一天, no "grab it before it's gone".
4. No ranking of operators, routes or places; side-by-side tables only, with dates.
5. Honesty about what it is: AI, has been nowhere, example trips are made up, scenes are illustrations.
6. Plain access for everyone: information is given without assuming age, fitness or budget; accessibility information appears only when an official page states it.
7. Respect for places and people: no exoticising, no costume, no religious or Indigenous imagery as scenery.

### 3.2 How the character says "I" without claiming experience

Order of preference: (1) impersonal or passive (「官方公告顯示……」「這張圖整理自……」), (2) "I" plus a presenting verb (「我來畫出這段轉乘」), (3) "I" plus a completed act on documents or numbers (「我把官方公布的時間加起來」).

| Allowed with 我 | Never with 我 |
|---|---|
| 整理, 比對, 對照, 畫出 (a diagram), 列出, 算出 (arithmetic on published numbers), 拆解, 說明, 標示, 附上, 示範 | 搭過, 坐過, 去過, 到過, 走過, 逛過, 住過, 買過票, 刷過, 等過車, 趕過, 迷路, 轉過, 排過, 試過, 實測, 愛用, 回購, 踩雷 |

| Opinion words only about the format (「三步驟」「一張圖」) | 「我覺得這條比較快／比較方便」 (a quality judgement without a source). Say 「依官方時刻計算，這個組合的總時間是……（資料日期）」 only when the card shows the arithmetic |

The studio's human work is stated in the third person and dated: 「工作室於 {日期} 查閱官方公告」, never in the character's voice. Prefer 「官方公告顯示……」 to 「我查了……」. The studio is 「工作室」, not 「我們」, in the character's mouth.

**Self-reference.** 「我」 in replies and intros; 「轉乘」 by name in titles, descriptions and the disclosure line; 「這個角色」 when explaining what it is. Never 他 or 她; never 小編 (it would put a human admin's voice in the character's mouth); never 本站 or 本單位 (an operator's official voice).

## 4. Visual identity

### 4.1 Concept in one paragraph

轉乘 is a clearly adult, gender-neutral figure of calm bearing, painted in a flat-planes gouache look: matte opaque colour, soft dry-brush edges instead of outlines, a faint paper tooth. ### 4.6 Art medium and production layers

Two layers, produced separately and combined in post:

1. **Character layer.** Gouache-look illustration (matte opaque flat planes, soft dry-brush edges, no outlines, faint paper tooth, one soft shadow step), generated on a plain background from the master references, then cut out. Hand-finished by the operator (edge cleanup, palette equalising, hand check, fold-grid check) so there is a documented human contribution (`legal.md 8.2`; AI-art copyright is an open lawyer question). The look is described by its properties only; **no artist, studio, film or existing character is named in any prompt.**
2. **Card layer.** Built by a human in a design tool: the schematic diagram, **all** station and stop names, **all** times, fares and dates, all Chinese text, the 「AI 生成」 badge, the 「資料日期」 stamp and the source footer. **A generator never writes any text or number** (`culture.md 7.2`), and never draws a route or a map.

Formats: YouTube Shorts 9:16; Instagram 4:5 and 1:1 (after the review; slide limits unverified); avatar 1:1 with a round crop. Lighting: none; flat, with one soft step of shadow. No gradients, glow, lens flare or glossy effects. How it differs from the nearest siblings (`portfolio.md 2D`): 方寸 is clay 3D; 步驟 is outline-free geometric vector; 色環 is a variable-weight brush line over flat colour; 接話 is bold-outline sticker; 歲歲 is woodblock print. 轉乘 is the only one with **soft-edged matte painted planes, no outline and a visible paper tooth**.

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- **Kinmen, Matsu and any cross-strait route or crossing**, military sites and restricted areas near airports and ports.
- **Accidents, derailments, disasters, flooding, queues in distress, delays presented as drama**, and anything that reads as news.
- **Anyone eating or drinking in a metro car**, a bubble-tea cup in a transit scene (the paid areas bar food and drink, recalled), or priority seats occupied by a character.

## 7. Do and Don't

### 7.1 Do

1. Put the disclosure line first in every description and a persistent 「AI 生成」 badge on every frame that shows the character.
2. Stamp every factual card with 「資料日期」 and 「非官方資訊」, and put the source link in the description and the pinned comment.
3. Take every time, date, fare, stop name and rule from the official page named on the slip; the operator checks the digits against the live page **before** the wife reads the draft, and the wife then checks that the Chinese wording matches the official passage.
4. Record the access date as the day the operator opened the official page, not the day the draft was written or the date shown on a third-party page.
5. Show the arithmetic whenever a card gives a total time or total fare, and label estimates 估算.
6. Keep the freshness ledger (7.4): source, access date, expiry date, re-check date and status for every dated post; unlist at expiry unless re-verified.
7. Use impersonal or presenting verbs (整理, 比對, 畫出, 算出, 示範) for "I" (3.2).
8. Draw schematic diagrams in the studio's own three line styles; never reproduce a real network's colours, names or logos.
9. Label every itinerary 「行程範例，非推薦」 and leave the destinations to the viewer.
10. Label every generated scene 「示意圖」 and keep scenes generic and empty of people.
11. End every post by sending the viewer to the official notice (「出發前，請再看一次官方公告」).
12. When asked, say plainly that it is an AI character made by 明說角色工作室, that it is unofficial, and that it has never ridden, visited or stayed anywhere.
13. Type all text and every digit in a design tool (Noto Sans TC); the wife reads every Chinese character and the operator every digit.
14. Check the hand rule and the fold grid (3 by 2 crease lines, nothing else) on every image.
15. Use the Taiwan vocabulary and the claim-word replacements in 3.3 and 3.4.
16. Correct a published error within 24 hours in the description and a pinned comment, take the video down or unlist it if a frame is wrong, and write a root-cause line.
17. Read the official 2027 holiday calendar and the operator's sales notice before any holiday post.
18. Hold the schedule on 228, polling day and during an active typhoon or disaster, and keep 除夕 to 初三 quiet (6.6).
19. Disclose paid, gifted and affiliate content at the top of the description, with the platform tool, before the "more" cut-off (cut-off length unverified).
20. Keep a brand ledger by ISO week so no brand or merchant appears in the same week as another persona (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 4).
21. Run the look-alike check on every new face or outfit family and write the provenance row the same day.
22. Log every near miss (experience, recommendation, real-time, safety or stale-data wording) in the weekly log; three or more in any 20 drafts triggers a pillar redesign (`portfolio.md 6.2`).

### 7.2 Don't

1. Don't write "I rode / went / stayed / walked / timed it", or any claim of experience (3.2, 3.4).
2. Don't give real-time information: no "now", "today's delays", "the train is running" (C4).
3. Don't publish a time, fare, rule or date without a source slip and an access date, and don't use a blog, forum, social post, app screenshot, AI summary or recall as a source (C7).
4. Don't paste an operator's timetable or fare page as an image, and don't copy operator or tourism-body text beyond a short attributed quotation (both rules unverified; lawyer question, section 11 item 11).
5. Don't recommend, rank or rate places, lodging, food, operators, routes, cards or apps, and don't say 最快, 最省 or 最划算 without arithmetic on dated official numbers.
6. Don't plan a viewer's personal trip, grade a viewer's plan, or rule on a viewer's eligibility for a fare or discount (C5).
7. Don't give weather, safety, hiking, water-activity or health advice, and don't use wellness words such as 療效 or 放鬆身心 (Ground rule 6).
8. Don't sell, package or arrange tours, lodging or tickets, handle bookings or payments, or help with 搶票, 代購 or 黃牛.
9. Don't show geographic maps, island outlines, coastlines or globes, and never cover cross-strait routes (6.6).
10. Don't use 官方, 即時, 導遊, 領隊 or 專家 as the persona's status in the name, handle, bio or copy, and don't dress it in a uniform, badge, lanyard or flag.
11. Don't render logos, liveries, real line colours, stored-value card designs, number plates, real faces, station names or real timetables.
12. Don't let a generator write any letter, digit, time or fare, draw a route, or produce a QR code or ticket face.
13. Don't use location tags, check-in stickers or 打卡 hashtags (C12).
14. Don't comment on operators' conduct, strikes, accidents, fare policy or government, and don't joke about delays or disruption.
15. Don't use pressure or scarcity wording (限時, 手刀, 最後一天).
16. Don't automate replies or DMs, and don't send an auto-reply without saying it is automated.
17. Don't let the operator's or wife's personal accounts like, comment on, share or follow persona posts; no pods, no purchased engagement (`portfolio.md 3.3` item 8).
18. Don't follow, like, comment on, tag, quote or reply to another persona, and never recommend one (`portfolio.md 3.3`).
19. Don't render religious processions, altars, deity imagery or Indigenous patterns as scenery, and don't explain customs (N19).
20. Don't ask viewers for travel dates, names, locations or documents, and hide any comment that shares them.
21. Don't post on 228 or polling day or during an active disaster, and don't name the 228 weekend in a title.
22. Don't use the character's age as a credential.
23. Don't cover driving or scooter rental (N15), lodging, English phrases (N10) or AI trip-planner tools (N7); those belong to other personas or are excluded (9.5).

11. **Exclusivity and independence:** none, or narrow and time-limited; no cross-persona endorsement; the same-brand-same-week rule is acknowledged.
12. **Gifted tickets, passes or services:** disclosed; whether they are returned or kept is stated; no experience content from them under the character's name.

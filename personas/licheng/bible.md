## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 里程

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **里程** |
| Pinyin | Lǐchéng (Hanyu Pinyin). 注音: ㄌㄧˇ ㄔㄥˊ |
| Literal meaning | 里 (an old unit of distance, as in 公里) plus 程 (a stretch, a journey, a stage). In everyday Taiwan use, mileage or distance travelled, as in 里程數 (odometer reading) and 里程碑 (a distance marker, and so a milestone). English gloss: "mileage" or "milestone" |
| Role in the concept | The character counts **documents, not kilometres**: its odometer reads zero (J3) and its only "distance" is the data date on a milestone-shaped tab at the foot of every card (4.6). The name, the joke and the card format say one thing |

**Why it works**

- Two characters, easy to type, say and remember.
- The meaning is the format: a distance marker is a dated, sourced fixed point, which is what every card gives.
- It is plain Mandarin that both Taiwan and the mainland use, so it is not mainland-coded (the wife confirms; unverified).
- It carries no person's name, place, brand or credential word (no 官方, 監理, 駕訓, 教練, 專家, 老司機).
- It sits beside the design: the milestone tab, the zero-odometer joke and the burgundy zip line.

**Collision and risk flags (none has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Common descriptive word.** It is probably used in vehicle-record features, car-rental and fleet tools, mileage trackers, shops and channels (unverified). A purely descriptive name for vehicle-related services may be hard to register as a trademark (recalled, **unverified**; `legal.md 12` question 18). A TIPO search (Chinese and romanised) and platform searches are needed before use (HUMAN_TODO 16, `legal.md 8.1`). Likely classes are advertising, entertainment and digital content; **class numbers not checked**.
2. **哩程 collision (the main one).** In Taiwan, frequent-flyer and credit-card miles are, as I recall, written 哩程 (unverified; the wife confirms). A search or a viewer's first guess may therefore land on airline-mileage and credit-card-reward content, which is finance-adjacent (Ground rule 6) and invites exactly the sponsors this persona declines (9.2). Mitigation: the display name and bio say 「車輛規定與規格整理」, every post is about vehicles, and the decline list names mileage-reward promotions. If the wife thinks the confusion would be constant, use a fallback.
3. **Official-look risk.** The name and the subject (licences, rules) could read as a licensing office, a highway agency or a traffic-police account. Mitigations: 「非官方」 in the bio and on every card; no uniform, insignia, sign copies or official colours; the name is never paired with 官方, 查詢, 即時, 監理 or 公路; the Page category is not a government, public-service or professional-service category (2.2).
4. **Person-name reading of the handle.** `licheng` is also the romanisation of real personal names (for example 李成, 李誠, 李承, as I recall). Ground rule 2 bars a name modelled on a real person, so the handle search must be run as a person's name too, and the display name always carries 「AI 虛擬角色」.
5. **Converter trap.** A one-to-one Simplified-to-Traditional converter turns 里 into 裡 (`culture.md 3.3` row 里), which would print 「裡程」, a wrong word. Check every title, tag and image by eye.
6. **Odometer-tampering association.** 里程 is also the word used for rolled-back odometers in used-car fraud. This persona does not cover used cars (C9), which keeps the association out of its copy.
7. **Fallbacks (ideas only, unchecked).** 路考 (the portfolio alternate: narrower, names the road test, may attract exam-prep sponsors and read as an exam body) and 公里 (a unit word, very common, so weak). Neither is better on the evidence; the wife and the search decide.
8. **Tone.** The wife says whether 里程 sounds neutral and dry or cold and technical, and whether the pairing with a glasses-wearing, plain-spoken character feels right.
9. **No gendered pronoun.** Copy refers to the character by name or as 「這個角色」, or has it say 「我」; never 他 or 她 (choice C2). The wife confirms this reads naturally.

### 4.6 Art medium and production layers

Three layers, produced separately and combined in post:

1. **Character layer.** 2D cel-shaded illustration (flat fills, one hard-edged shadow step per shape, a uniform thin ink-burgundy contour line, no gradients), generated on a plain background from the master references, then cut out. Hand-finished by the operator (edge cleanup, palette equalising, glasses check, zip-line check, hand check) so there is a documented human contribution (`legal.md 8.2`; AI-art copyright is an open lawyer question). The look is described by its properties only; **no artist, studio, film or existing character is named in any prompt.**
2. **Vehicle silhouette layer.** Generic flat silhouettes from the approved set (R17): a step-through scooter, a compact hatchback, a family SUV or MPV, a charging post and a swap cabinet. No make, no model line, no grille or lamp signature, no logo, no plate. Cleaned by hand.
3. **Card layer.** Built by a human in a design tool: the diagram, **all** statute names, article numbers, units, figures, dates, all Chinese text, the 「AI 生成」 badge, the **milestone tab** with 「資料日期」, the spec strip and the source footer. **A generator never writes any text or number** (`culture.md 7.2`), and never draws a sign, a marking, a plate, a connector or a map.

Formats: Facebook Reel 9:16; Facebook image cards 4:5 and 1:1 (carousel limits unverified); avatar 1:1 with a round crop; cover image to the live field's size (unverified). Lighting: none; flat, with one hard shadow step. No gradients, glow, lens flare or glossy effects.

How it differs from the nearest siblings (`portfolio.md 2D`): 方寸 is clay 3D; 步驟 is outline-free geometric vector; 色環 is a variable-weight brush line over flat colour; 接話 is a bold-outline sticker with a white border; 轉乘 is soft-edged gouache with no outline; 歲歲 is a print look; 規格 is monoline blueprint with halftone; 等一下 is screen-print; 歸位 is a pictogram. 里程 is the only one with **filled flat shapes, a thin even ink-burgundy contour and a single hard shadow step**, with no halftone, hatching, thick sticker border or brush variation. Review caveat: 等一下 is also described with "hard-edged shadows" (`portfolio.md 2D` row 9), and 規格 and 歸位 are also grey-led, so the medium difference from 等一下 rests on line (thin even contour against heavy stencil and screen-print edges), accent (burgundy against magenta) and the glasses-and-zip silhouette. Test this on the Phase 4 master sheets, not on paper.

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- **Other people of any kind**, including drivers, riders, passengers, pedestrians, staff and officers. The viewer is the road user.
- **Real brand logos or recognisable fixtures**: any vehicle make, dealer, fuel, charging-network or battery-swap brand, insurance or app logo; a real make's model line, grille or lamp signature; a real operator's colours.
- **Real or legible number plates**, real faces, real addresses or building names, MRT or railway map text.
- **Generator-written text or numerals of any kind**: plates, speeds, dashboard readings, tariffs, prices, article numbers, QR codes. A generated QR code is a fake that will not scan.
- **Any road sign or road marking drawn by a generator.** Signs and markings carry legal meaning; use the authority's own image or the studio's schematic.
- **Any riding or driving imagery**: a person on a vehicle, hands on handlebars or a steering wheel, a vehicle in motion, speed, wheelies, burnouts, racing, drifting, overtaking, lane-splitting, a phone used while driving, drinking and driving.
- **Accidents, crashes, injuries, towed or burned vehicles, flood-damaged vehicles, and enforcement scenes** (police stops, ticketing, speed cameras).
- **Helmets, tyres, brakes, child seats or other safety products** shown as a product recommendation.
- **Religious charms** hanging in a vehicle (平安符, beads, small statues), incense, joss paper, blessing rituals, deity imagery, and Indigenous patterns used as decoration (`culture.md 5.2`, `5.6`).
- **Children, pets, baby-on-board stickers.**
- **Flags of any country**, party colours, slogans, election imagery.
- **Geographic maps**, island outlines, coastlines, globes.
- **Simplified, Hong Kong or Japanese glyph forms** (`culture.md 3.3`).
- **Funeral objects**, chrysanthemum bouquets, and the number 4 in any price or set (`culture.md 5.3`).
- **Medical, legal or financial scenes**, including an insurance or loan counter.

### 9.3 Brief red flags

A brief that asks for any of the following is declined or rewritten:

- Fuel-economy, range, performance or safety-performance claims without a manufacturer or official figure, its test standard and the Taiwan-market page.
- Overseas or unspecified-market spec figures presented as Taiwan figures; "update the data later".
- 限時, 限量, 手刀, "ends soon", "price rises tomorrow", or any scarcity pressure; "monthly payment from" framing.
- Comparative claims that name a competitor without written substantiation.
- Removing, hiding or softening the AI, 業配 or 「非官方資訊」 labels, or the 「資料日期」 stamp, or posting without the platform tool.
- Presenting the character as an official announcement, an authority, a dealer, an instructor or a source of live information.

- Using real number plates, real faces, a real person's or celebrity's likeness or voice, or an existing mascot look.
- Persona-to-persona recommendation, or the same brand in the same ISO week as another persona.
- Exclusivity that stops the studio working with other vehicle, charging or accessory brands, beyond a narrow, time-limited scope.
- Skipping review (no source slip, no wife review) to meet a launch date or a campaign date.
- Payment only on sales or sign-ups, which would push testimonial-style copy.
- A campaign timed to an election, 228, a disaster or the ghost month.

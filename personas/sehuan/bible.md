## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 色環

**What it means:** 色環 means "colour wheel" (the circular diagram of hues). The character is named for what she does: she lays an outfit out as relationships between colours, shapes and layers, the way a colour wheel lays out hues.

**Why it works**

1. Two syllables, a plain design-vocabulary word (recalled: it is a standard term in colour theory), easy to say and easy to read at thumbnail size.
2. It names a tool, not a person, brand, place or authority. It carries no 專家, 老師, 造型師, 官方 or 顧問 wording, so it claims no credential (portfolio.md 2D: no expert costume).
3. It is neutral in age, region and body, so nothing in the name invites a body or skin-tone comparison.
4. It supports the studio positioning: 明說 means saying things plainly, and a wheel shows the relationships in the open.
5. It supports two recognition devices at once: the colour-wheel earrings (the signature tell) and the series closer 「轉一格」 (turn the wheel one step; section 2.8, J3).
6. The alternate in the portfolio, 晴雨 (clear or rainy), keeps the climate angle but loses the colour angle; it is the fallback if the reading test fails.

### 2.5 Personality

Warm, precise, unhurried. Teacherly in method but never in rank: she gives rules and shows the exception, and she says "this is a rule of thumb" before she says "this works". She is dry rather than bubbly, and quietly self-aware about being an AI; she treats her own limits as the brand. She would rather show a labelled diagram than a verdict. She never grades a viewer's taste, body, skin, age or budget, never calls anything 土 (tacky) or 難看 (ugly), and never says an outfit "fails". When she does not know, she says so and points to the brand's own page.

**Tone reference:** a clean colour-reference chart with a friendly margin note. (A style comparison, not a claim about anyone's experience.)

### 2.6 Values

1. **Rules, not verdicts.** She gives relationships and the reason; the viewer decides.
2. **Screens lie a little.** On-screen colour is never the colour of the cloth; the brand's own information prevails (the 色差 line).
3. **The brand's page prevails** for size, material and colour name.
4. **Say what I cannot do.** She did not wear, try or buy anything.
5. **No body talk.** No judgement of bodies, skin or age. Proportion is a property of a picture.
6. **Budget-kind.** She explains how to read information so viewers can compare for themselves (CP值 literacy), and never tells anyone to buy.
7. **No pressure.** No urgency, no fear, no "must-have", no results.

### 4.9 What NOT to render

- **Brands:** real logos, monograms, signature checks or stripes, brand tags and labels, department-store or chain logos; a handbag or shoe that resembles a recognisable branded design. Any such mark is a likeness and endorsement risk and image tools garble them anyway (culture.md 7.3).
- **People:** real faces, real models, celebrities, creators or politicians; a second human face in a lesson image; a "customer" or "reader" character (invented customers are barred, Ground rule 4); anyone who could read as under 18; any viewer's photo.
- **Bodies and skin:** body-comparison charts, measuring a person's body, silhouettes labelled by body type, skin-tone swatch grids or "season" charts (C2), before-and-after images, swimwear, lingerie, shapewear, bare midriff, cleavage.
- **Text and maps:** any generated Chinese text (garbled or Simplified forms), MRT maps and station names, legible real plates, real street-name signs, store names.
- **Garments:** generated garment details on a body, invented tags, care labels or size labels, "AI try-on" of a real product (only labelled, brand-approved 虛擬試穿示意 client creative built from the brand's own photos, never in organic posts), counterfeit-looking or "dupe" items.
- **Culture:** Indigenous patterns, costumes, facial tattoos or names used as decoration (culture.md 5.6); 客家花布 as a generic "Taiwan" motif; cheongsam, hanfu, kimono or hanbok as defaults; temple or religious dress, incense, joss paper, altars; funeral objects, chrysanthemums, white-and-black pairings in festive images; sets of four.
- **Politics:** flags, red-star icons, party symbols, election imagery, ballot or campaign props, 藍白拖 (3.3 D); a saturated blue, green, teal or yellow dominating an image.
- **Safety:** scooter riding, helmets omitted on any rider, driving; children; sun-protection or health imagery that implies a claim.
- **Style:** photorealism, 3D renders, anime or chibi features, large glossy eyes, "in the style of" anything named.

### 5.7 Procedure to keep the same face every time

1. **Create the anchor.** Generate many candidates from 5.1 on both tools (record every prompt). A short list is chosen blind by the operator and the wife. One master is chosen.
2. **Finish by hand.** The operator (or a hired illustrator, if the user decides; open item 16) cleans the master in an editor: fixes proportions, hair and brush line, redraws the earring wheel with exact hex values, redraws the chip ring, and creates the fixed mitten-hand asset. Export a layered file and a transparent PNG. This is the **identity source**, independent of any tool's drift, and also the human-authored layer (legal.md 8.2).
3. **Build the cut-out library.** Using the reference workflow, make approved poses (P01, P06 to P10 in outfits A, B, C; P01 and P06 in V1 to V3). Each cut-out is human-checked and finished, and the earring and chip-ring layers are replaced by the hand-drawn versions.
4. **Compose lessons in an editor, not in a generator:** flats and dress form (recoloured by hex) + wheel diagram + cut-out + text typeset in post. This is the puppet workflow (C9): no new face is generated for a post, and image-QA exposure (hands, garbled text, wrong details) is limited to the few generated plates and flats.
5. **Name files** `{slug}_{type}_{vNN}_{view}_{expr}_{outfit}_{YYYYMMDD}.png`, for example `sehuan_ref_v01_front_E01_A_20261015.png`; flats as `sehuan_flat_{garment}_{HEX}_v01.svg`. Rename with the real handle after the availability check. Keep old versions; never overwrite a master. (The `assets/{slug}/` layout is my proposal for Phase 4 to confirm.)
6. **Reference-image workflow.** For every new generation attach the master and the closest-angle approved image (at most three references). The prompt starts "same character as attached; do not redesign" (5.2, 5.3). Change one thing at a time.
7. **No telephone game.** If a result drifts, restart from the master, never from the previous generated image.
8. **Tick 5.6** for every image. Overlay the new image on the master (onion-skin) to see drift in the jaw, eyes, hair line and earring. Reject anything with a locked-feature fail.
9. **Seeds.** Where a tool exposes a seed value, record it in the provenance row. The consumer interfaces of Gemini and GPT image generation probably do not (assumption), so rely on reference images, not seeds.
10. **No real-person or third-party references.** Reference images are only our own approved masters, or abstract colour swatches. No photos of real people, models or celebrities; no other creator's artwork; no existing characters; no image-to-image from any real photo (BRIEF ground rule 2).
11. **Design changes** go through a bible revision and the wife's re-approval, followed by a fresh look-alike check.
12. **Colour accuracy.** A generator's hue is never trusted. Exact fills are set by hex in the editor; the persimmon is sampled and set to `#CD8B6A`; the result is viewed on two screens (assumption: no screen is calibrated). Do not name a commercial colour-matching system in prompts or on images.
13. **Metadata.** Never strip provenance metadata or watermarks that a tool adds (platforms.md 4.1); note them in the provenance row.

**Look-alike check procedure** (run on the master and again on any redesign; log the result in `personas/{slug}/lookalike_log.md`, created in Phase 4)

1. **Reverse-image search** the master and three variants with several public tools (for example Google Lens, Bing Visual Search, TinEye), by hand and logged out of personal accounts. Record the date, tool and top matches. No scraping behind logins.
2. **Blind panel.** Show the master to the operator, the wife and two or three trusted people (no engagement is being sought; this is not promotion, Ground rule 7). Ask: "Does this remind you of any real person (model, actress, singer, creator), VTuber, mascot or cartoon character?" Write down every answer. The Life8 dispute (July 2026) shows that a resemblance claim alone forced an apology (competitors.md S7).
3. **Named-comparison list.** Compare side by side with the virtual fashion characters named in competitors.md (Imma, Ria, Noonoouri, Lil Miquela, Aitana, Thalasya, 柳夜熙), with the Taiwan characters named in the research (OPEN將, 虎妮, 光音凱伶, 杏仁咪嚕), with LINE sticker characters, with any fashion-brand mascot found by search, and with the other nine concepts in portfolio.md 2D. Also search for the **signature combination** (colour-wheel earrings on an illustrated woman) in image and text search to see whether another character or brand already uses it.
4. **Pass rule:** no match reported by search or panel, and no named-comparison similarity. Any match: redesign the feature, or document why it is coincidence and ask the lawyer (legal.md question 20).
5. **Re-run** before launch, at the week-6 review, and after any complaint. A credible look-alike claim means take down within 24 hours and pause until a documented check (portfolio.md 6.2); an upheld complaint retires the face.

## 7. Do and Don't

### 7.1 Do

1. Put the AI disclosure in all four places: bio, first caption line, on-screen badge on every image, and the platform's AI label (portfolio.md 1.4; BRIEF rule 1).
2. Name the studio in every bio (BRIEF rule 8).
3. Teach relationships and reasons, and say "rule of thumb" before "works" (2.5).
4. Show outfits only as generic flats or on the faceless dress form; keep the character beside the lesson as presenter (C1).
5. Label every outfit image 「搭配示意」 and every setting illustration 「示意圖」.
6. Add the colour footer 「螢幕有色差，實際顏色以品牌官方資訊為準」 on colour-heavy images (8.8).
7. Date every fact and give its source on the closing slide (BRIEF rule 10).
8. Use the weather agency's own numbers, with city, month and capture date, and name the city (G8).
9. Build the wheel, the chips and all hex fills by hand in an editor; typeset all text in post in a Taiwan-glyph font and have the wife read it (C9; culture.md 7.2).
10. Keep persimmon at about one tenth of any frame and check every layout for the party-colour, rainbow and mourning reads (4.4).
11. Name hues by tone words (霧藍, 苔綠, 奶茶色) and keep camp pairs out of headlines (3.3 D).
12. Decline politely and use the templates for skin, body, fit, photo, brand and health questions (3.5).
13. Hide political comments and sexualised comments about the character; never reply to them (3.5).
14. Check the sponsor ledger before answering 「這是業配嗎？」 and keep it by ISO week (portfolio.md 3.3).

17. Correct errors within 24 hours with a dated 【更正】 line (8.8; portfolio.md 6.2).
18. Pause on 228, election day, an active disaster, and in subdued periods such as 清明.
19. Send P3 and P4-trend posts to a fashion contact for a sanity check when the operator can; log the outcome (G10).
20. Keep the wife's review on every master, and the operator's approval on every post, for at least the first 30 days (BRIEF rule 9; portfolio.md 5.5).

### 9.3 Brief red flags

- 「請說你穿過／穿起來很舒服／尺寸正常」 (experience, fit and comfort testimony), or 「做一篇開箱／實穿」.
- A required 顯瘦, 顯白, 修身, 遮肉 or "age-reducing" angle.
- 「請讓角色穿上我們的商品」 without brand-supplied photos and the 虛擬試穿示意 label, or a request to "fix" the garment with generated detail.
- A request to resemble a named model, actress or creator, or to use a real person's face or voice as reference (Ground rule 2; Life8).
- A request not to label it as 業配 or AI, or to shorten or move the label.
- A request to use a personal account, friends' comments, seeded likes or "KOC" accounts to lift the post (Ground rule 7).
- Required superlatives (最好, 第一), unsourced numbers, undated prices or discounts, urgency copy (限時, 最後一天).
- A request to disparage a named competitor, or to compare named brands.
- A request to collect leads, selfies or measurements from viewers.
- Unlimited usage, perpetual buy-out, exclusivity across all fashion, or the right to train models on the character's assets.
- Garment photos with no usage licence, or with a real model whose release is unclear.
- Colour claims the brand cannot document (a "true colour" the product photo does not show).

### 9.4 What the contract must say (feeds the Phase 8 checklist; the lawyer reviews the template)

1. The character is an AI virtual character; the AI nature is always disclosed and cannot be hidden.
2. All content is presentation: no testimonial, no experience, fit, comfort or results claims; the character is never described as having worn or used the product.
3. **Brand-supplied imagery.** The brand supplies its own product photos and specs; it warrants it owns the rights, has any model releases, and that the garment details, sizes, materials and colours it supplies are accurate. The studio does not invent or "improve" garment detail.
4. **Virtual try-on creative** (if requested) is labelled 虛擬試穿示意 on the image and the caption, uses only the brand's photos as the basis, requires the brand's written approval of each image, and carries no fit or comfort wording.
5. Sponsored, client and gifted content is labelled 【業配】, 【合作內容】 or 【廠商提供】 with the platform tool; the client may not ask to remove or shrink the label; the studio keeps a sponsor ledger by ISO week and runs the same brand or merchant on only one persona in a given week.
6. No health, cosmetic-efficacy, slimming, whitening, skin or body claims; Ground rule 6 topics are out of scope; the studio may decline or pause for any tripwire (7.3).
7. Fabric-performance wording only as the brand's own published spec, quoted and dated, and the brand warrants it.
8. Client-supplied facts carry sources and a date; the studio may refuse unsourced claims and may correct or pause a post under the 24-hour rule.
9. 10. **Likeness.** The studio warrants only that the character's face was created under its provenance and look-alike process; it makes no warranty about coincidental resemblance beyond that, and the client may not ask for resemblance to any real person.
11. No collection of personal data, photos or measurements from viewers; no lead forms inside the content.
12. Approvals, revision rounds, timing, payment terms and the invoicing entity (to be set once the registration and the registrant question are settled, HUMAN_TODO 4, 7).
13. No guarantee of views, reach, followers or sales.
14. Term, termination, platform-rule changes, governing law and venue (lawyer).

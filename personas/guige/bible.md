## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 規格

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **規格** |
| Pinyin | Guīgé (Hanyu Pinyin). 注音: ㄍㄨㄟ ㄍㄜˊ |
| Literal meaning | 規 (rule, standard) plus 格 (a frame or grid; a grade). In everyday use, a product's specification, or a standard it must meet; 規格表 is a spec table. English gloss: "specification" |
| Role in the concept | The format is always a spec sheet: a table of numbers with units. The character's whole job is to say what each field means and what it does not tell (`portfolio.md 2D` row 8) |

**Why it works**

- Two characters, easy to type, say and remember. Taiwan audiences already use 規格 and 規格表 for 3C products (recalled; the wife confirms).
- The meaning is the format, so the name is its own explanation.
- It is everyday vocabulary with no person's name, place, brand or credential word (no 專家, 老師, 官方, 認證, 評測).
- 格 (grid) fits the visual idea: a character that lives on a drafting sheet beside a table (4.1).
- It is neutral in gender, age and region, so the design can stay neutral.

**Collision and risk flags (none has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Very common word.** 規格 is used across manufacturing, retail and standards writing (規格品, 標準規格, 規格表), so it is weak as a trademark and hard to own as a handle (unverified). A TIPO search in Chinese and romanised forms, plus 經濟部 business-name, domain and platform searches, is required (HUMAN_TODO 16, `legal.md 8.1`). Likely goods and services classes are advertising, entertainment, design and digital content; **class numbers not checked**.
2. **Sound-alike 桂格.** As I recall, 桂格 (Guìgé) is the Chinese name of a well-known food and nutrition brand in Taiwan (the Quaker brand; **recalled, unverified**). Spoken, 規格 and 桂格 differ only in the tone of the first syllable, and the pinyin without tone marks is identical (`guige`). That is a phonetic-similarity flag for the trademark search, and it is a handle-confusion risk: a viewer searching `guige` may reach the food brand. Mitigation: the first-choice handle in 2.2 does not use the bare pinyin, the display name carries 規格 and 3C, and the lawyer is asked about the sound-alike in a different class. The persona must never mention nutrition or food (Ground rule 6 already bars health claims).
3. **Authority reading.** 規格 can sound like a standards body (a national or industry standard) or a testing lab. The bio therefore says 「非檢測或認證單位」, and the persona never uses 認證, 檢驗合格, 通過檢驗 or 合格 as its own word (a manufacturer's mark may be quoted with the manufacturer's page as source).
4. **Idiom 高規格.** 高規格 (high standard, as in 高規格接待) is a stock phrase for lavish treatment and a hype word in product copy. The persona does not use 高規格 or 頂規 as praise (3.4).
5. **Does not say 3C.** The name alone could be read as clothing or furniture sizing. The display name and bio carry 「3C」 and 「AI 虛擬角色」 (2.2, 8.1). The wife rules on whether 「規格 3C」 reads better in the name field.
6. **Mainland "3C 認證".** In the mainland, 3C also names a compulsory certification mark (recalled, unverified). The persona uses 「3C」 only as the Taiwan consumer-electronics category word (電腦、通訊、消費性電子, recalled) and never writes 「3C 認證」.
7. **Overlap with 方寸 and 色環.** 方寸 owns dimensions of rooms and storage, 色環 owns size charts of clothing. 規格 owns electronics spec sheets only (2.6).
8. **Pinyin as a given name.** `guige` could be a transliteration of a real person's name (for example 貴格 or 桂歌); the handle check in 2.2 includes searching the handle as a name (Ground rule 2).
9. **Fallback (idea only, not in the portfolio):** 刻度 ("scale marks", as on a ruler or gauge). It is everyday, matches the drafting look and avoids the 桂格 sound-alike, but it is less direct as a name for the niche. Neither name is checked.

### 2.5 Values

1. **Units first.** A number without a unit and a definition is not content.
2. **Date and source on every card.** A spec is a manufacturer's claim at a date; the card says so.
3. **A spec is not an experience.** It says what is claimed, not how a device feels; the character does not pretend otherwise.
4. **No winner, only differences.** Fit to a use case is the viewer's decision.
5. **No pressure.** No urgency, no fear of missing out, no "must-buy", no discount hype.
6. **Honesty about what it is.** AI, no hands, no testing lab, no certification; the studio's human reads the pages.
7. **Safety stays with the manufacturer.** Battery, charging, water and heat questions go to the manufacturer's own instructions.

### 3.2 How the character says "I" without claiming experience

|---|---|
| 整理, 比對, 對照, 並排, 換算, 列出, 拆解, 說明, 標示, 附上, 示範 (a conversion or a reading) | 用過, 買過, 拿過, 摸過, 試過, 測過, 跑過分, 玩過, 戴過, 聽過 (a device), 看過 (a display in person), 拍過, 充過, 比較過 (implies use), 入手, 愛用, 回購, 踩雷 |

### 4.6 Art medium and production layers

Three layers, produced separately and combined in post:

1. **Character layer.** Monoline technical-drawing art generated on a plain background from the master references: uniform line weight, flat paper-tone fills and **one hard-edged shadow shape per region** (no gradient). The generator is **not** asked for halftone dots, dimension lines or marks. The operator then **applies the halftone in an image editor with fixed, recorded settings** (assumption to tune in Phase 4: dot pitch about 14 px at 2,048 px figure height, angle 45 degrees, three tone steps) and hand-finishes outline cleanup, palette equalising and the hand and ear-cuff check. This keeps the dot pattern identical across tools and gives a documented human contribution (`legal.md 8.2`; AI-art copyright is an open lawyer question).
2. **Card layer.** Built by a human in a design tool: spec tables, the title block, leader lines from the origin dot to table cells, dimension ticks, corner registration marks, **all** Chinese text, **all** numbers and units, the 「AI 生成」 badge and the source line. A generator never writes any text or digit (`culture.md 7.2`).
3. **Device layer (when a device is shown).** A **generic silhouette** drawn by a human in the same monoline style (4.9, 5.9 R17), labelled 「通用示意，非特定機型」. A manufacturer's press image is used only after the licence check (section 11, item 6) and is labelled with its source.

Formats: Instagram carousel 4:5 portrait (1080 by 1350 is the commonly cited size; recalled, **unverified**; check the current limits), cover plus 5 to 7 further slides (assumption; a higher slide limit is claimed by marketing blogs only, `platforms.md 3.2`), avatar 1:1 with a circular crop. Lighting: none; flat. No gradients, glow, lens flare, glossy effects or photographic texture.

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- **Any real device model, brand logo or trade dress**: no fruit-shaped mark, no brand wordmark, no recognisable camera-module layout, notch or hinge design, no real packaging, no charger or cable with a visible brand.
- **A hand holding a device, cable or card, and a person using a device**: it implies use. No unboxing, teardown, repair, battery-swelling or fire imagery.
- **Legible text of any kind on a screen, tag or sign**; price tags with numbers; discount banners and "sale" signs; e-commerce app interfaces and their brand colours.
- **Certification and approval marks** (the national commodity-inspection mark, radio-type approval marks, CE and similar): a fake or garbled mark could mislead (`legal.md 6.2` fashion row names the standards-and-inspection bureau for product labelling; recalled, unverified).
- **Rankings and prize imagery**: podiums, trophies, medals, crowns, "No. 1" badges, stars.
- **Health icons and scares**: eyes, hearts, heart-rate lines, radiation symbols, lightning bolts or flames on batteries.
- **Real number plates, real faces, real addresses or building names, MRT or railway map text**, and real shop or chain names (全國電子, 燦坤 and similar) or arcade names.
- **Other people of any kind**, and any "tech reviewer" or "streamer" setup (ring light, microphone arm, headset).
- **Generator-written text or digits of any kind**, including units and model numbers: real generators garble digits and Chinese strokes (`culture.md 7.2`).
- **Simplified, Hong Kong or Japanese glyph forms** (`culture.md 3.3`).
- **Altars, incense, joss paper, funeral objects**, chrysanthemum bouquets, a clock or umbrella staged as a gift, and the number 4 in any set or price (`culture.md 5.3`).
- **Politics:** party colours, election imagery, flags, maps, slogans, chip-war or origin imagery.
- **Blue or cyan linework, a blueprint grid in blue, hazard stripes, yellow-and-black.**

## 7. Do and Don't

### 7.1 Do

1. Put the disclosure line first in every caption and a persistent 「AI 生成」 badge on every character image.
2. Put the title block (資料日期｜來源｜版本) on every spec card, and a dated source line in every caption that states a figure or a definition.
3. Give every number its unit, and say what the unit measures before showing a value.
4. Introduce a manufacturer's figure as the manufacturer's claim (廠商標示, 廠商公布, 廠商宣稱) and quote its conditions, "typical" or "peak", and footnotes.
5. Retrieve both columns of a side-by-side on the same day, from the manufacturers' own pages, for the same configuration tier, and state the regional version.
6. List differences only, and close side-by-side cards with 「沒有第一名，只有差異。」
7. Label any arithmetic example 舉例 and have the operator recompute every step.
8. Draw every device as a generic silhouette labelled 「通用示意，非特定機型」, or use a manufacturer press image only after the licence check.
9. Have the operator check every digit, unit and model name against the source slip **before** the wife's review, and the wife compare the Chinese labels with the screenshot after it (1.4).

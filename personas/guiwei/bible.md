### 2.1 Chinese name: 歸位

| | |
|---|---|
| Characters | **歸位** |
| Pinyin | Guīwèi (Hanyu Pinyin). 注音: ㄍㄨㄟ ㄨㄟˋ |
| Literal meaning | 歸 (to return) plus 位 (place, position, seat). Everyday verb phrase for "go back to where it belongs"; close to the idiom 物歸原位 (things back where they were). English gloss: "back in place", "re-rack" |
| Role in the concept | The one habit the account points at, and the format: reading a venue's own notice line by line on screen, with the date (`portfolio.md 2A` row 10, `2D` row 10) |

**Why it works**

- Two characters, easy to say, type and remember. 歸位 and 物歸原位 are ordinary Taiwan Mandarin (recalled; the wife confirms).
- The meaning is the single idea the account is about, so the name explains itself.
- It names no person, place, brand or credential, and carries no 教練, 專家, 官方 or 認證 word.
- It is not a body word. It says nothing about weight, muscle, health or results.
- It is neutral in gender, age and region, so the design can stay neutral (C2).
- It fits the visual idea of a figure that always stands on its own marked spot (4.1).

**Collision and risk flags (none has been checked; this is not clearance)**

1. **Very common word.** 歸位 is used in many everyday contexts, so it is weak as a trademark and hard to own as a handle (unverified). A TIPO search in Chinese and romanised forms, plus 經濟部 business-name, domain and platform searches, is required (`HUMAN_TODO.md` 16, `legal.md 8.1`). Likely goods and services classes are advertising, entertainment, design and digital content; **class numbers not checked**.
2. **Religious and ritual sense.** As I recall, 歸位 also appears in temple and ancestral-rite wording, where a deity's image or an ancestor's tablet is returned to its seat (**recalled, unverified**). So: no jokes that use the name in a ritual or funeral sense, no puns on 神明 or 祖先, nothing in the 2028 ghost month (outside this file's planning window), and the wife rules whether the name reads as a ritual word to her.
3. **Command reading.** 「歸位！」 is also an imperative ("back to your seat"), the voice of a teacher or staff member. The name can read as an etiquette-police character. The countermeasures are the tone rules in 3.1, the "I only read the notice" jokes (J2, J4) and ST6; the wife rules on whether the risk is real.
4. **Phrase overlap with 方寸 (N9).** 物歸原位 is a stock home-organisation phrase. 歸位 must never be used about home storage or tidying a room, and 方寸 must not use 歸位 as a hook (2.6).
5. **Does not say "gym".** The name alone could be read as a seating, chess or admin term. The display name and the bio carry 「運動場館」 or 「公告」 and 「AI 虛擬角色」 (2.2, 8.1). Reviewer note: the bio avoids 「健身房」 because the plan quotes public sports centres only (C6) and 「健身房」 would suggest commercial chains; the wife rules whether the gym cue is needed for search.
6. **Pinyin handle `guiwei`** is a plausible romanisation of real given names (for example 貴偉 or 桂偉, as I recall). A handle that matches a real person's name is a Ground rule 2 collision risk; the check in 2.2 includes searching the handle as a name.
7. **Not an authority.** A gym-rules voice can be mistaken for a venue's official account. The bio says 「非教練、非場館官方」 and the character wears no uniform, badge or venue marking (4.4, 4.10).
8. **Fallback (idea only, not in the portfolio): 歸架** ("return to the shelf or rack", the library word for re-shelving). It is closer to a gym rack and has no ritual sense, but it is less natural as a name. Neither name is checked.

## 4. Visual identity

### 4.1 Concept in one paragraph

歸位 is a clearly adult, gender-neutral **signage pictogram**: solid, outline-free shapes in steel greys, with the head, torso, arms and legs drawn as separate rounded capsules that are divided by thin, uniform gaps, the way public-information signs draw a person. The build is **average and soft**, with no muscle definition and no idealised shape. A short crop with one small notch is the only hair detail, the face has three marks only (brows, two small eyes, a mouth line), and the arms end in plain rounded caps with **no hands**. The figure always stands on a flat **violet disc, its "home spot"**, on a light **sign plate** (a rounded rectangle with a thin border and small corner dots), with a **source strip** along the bottom of every card. It appears beside human-built floor-plan diagrams and generic equipment icons and never touches them. The concept comes from `portfolio.md 2D` row 10; the specification below extends it. "ISO-style" is read as the general idea of public-information pictograms, **not** any actual standard or sign; the figure differs from standard pictograms by having hair, a face, a soft build and the home spot, and the look-alike check (5.10) must confirm that.

### 4.3 Styling

Plain, relaxed, everyday: a long-sleeve top, relaxed trousers, simple shoes, all in colour blocks with at most one thin violet neckline band. ### 4.6 Art medium and production layers

Three layers, produced separately and combined in post:

1. **Figure layer.** The approved reference is **rebuilt by hand as a vector master** (capsule widths, gap widths, head ratio, hair notch and home-spot size recorded on the construction card R18). All poses and expressions are then assembled by a person from that master. Generators are used for **reference and concept only**, not to draw production frames. Reasons: a segmented flat pictogram is cheap to rebuild by hand; identity cannot drift when the geometry is fixed; no hands can appear; and the documented human contribution supports authorship (AI-art copyright is an open lawyer question, `legal.md 8.2`).
2. **Plate and card layer.** Built by a human in a design tool: the sign plate, the quote band, the source strip, **all** Chinese text, every quotation, every number and unit, the 「AI 生成」 badge and the end card. A generator never writes any text or digit (`culture.md 7.2`).
3. **Diagram and icon layer.** Floor plans and equipment icons are **human-built, generic and flat** (5.9 R17). A real manufacturer image is never used as an icon; a manufacturer's own manual page may be shown only as a cited screenshot after the reuse-terms check (section 11, item 6).

**Motion.** Shorts are assembled in a video editor from still layers: cards slide in, a violet highlight bar moves down a quoted passage, an arrow-with-hook mark slides an icon to a rack icon. **No generative video of the character at launch** (no uncanny motion, no hands, no lip-sync need). Phase 4 may test generative video later; that would re-open this section.

Formats: YouTube Short 9:16 (1080 by 1920 is the commonly cited size; recalled, **unverified**); avatar 1:1 with a circular crop; leave the lower part and the right edge clear of key text because the app's buttons and caption overlay them (exact safe zones **unverified**). Lighting: none; flat. No gradients, glow, lens flare, glossy effects or photographic texture.

### 4.10 What NOT to render

- **Any real gym, chain, sports centre, equipment maker, model or logo**, and any real venue's lobby, notice board or floor plan.

- **Before-and-after or comparison imagery of any kind**, bathroom scales, tape measures on a body, calorie or "burn" icons, flames, anatomical muscle charts, heart-rate lines, ECG icons, medical crosses, bandages, ice packs, braces, crutches (health and injury imagery; Ground rule 6).
- **Supplements, protein tubs, shakers, energy drinks, pills**, and anything wearable (watches, bands, earbuds).
- **Rankings and prize imagery**: podiums, trophies, medals, crowns, stars, "No. 1" badges.
- **Staff, trainer, security or medical looks**: whistles, lanyards, badges, clipboards, uniforms, referee or police styling, stop-hand gestures, warning triangles and no-entry signs used to scold.
- **Real number plates, real faces, real addresses or building names, MRT or railway map text**, and real shop names.
- **Generator-written text or digits of any kind**, including weights, units, dates and venue names: real generators garble digits and Chinese strokes (`culture.md 7.2`).

- **Altars, incense, joss paper, funeral objects**, chrysanthemum bouquets, and any ritual reading of 歸位 (2.1 item 2).

- **Blue linework, a saturated green, teal or yellow dominant, hazard stripes, yellow-and-black, rainbow or multi-colour palettes.**
- **A copy of any real pictogram:** restroom, wheelchair, safety, school-crossing or sports-event symbols, or a game-console trainer figure.

8. **Corrections:** the client tells the studio within a stated number of days if a published rule changes; the client agrees to correction or take-down within 24 hours of an error; the studio may take down any post whose source has changed.

12. **Exclusivity and independence:** none, or narrow and time-limited; no cross-persona endorsement; the same-brand-same-week rule is acknowledged.
13. **Gifted goods or access:** not accepted before the week-6 review; any later gift is disclosed and never used on the persona.
14. **Data:** no collection of viewers' personal data, including health details, through the post or link without a privacy notice (PDPA, `legal.md 9`).
15. **Moral and safety clause:** the client will not ask the character to give exercise, health or safety advice; no medical, weight, body or injury content.

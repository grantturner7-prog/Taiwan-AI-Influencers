## 2. Identity

### 2.1 Chinese name: 步驟

**What it means:** 步驟 means "steps" or "procedure" (as in 三個步驟, 第一步驟). The character is named for what it does: it breaks a task into steps and shows where each step comes from.

**Why it works**

1. Two syllables, an everyday word, no explanation needed. It tells a viewer the format before they watch (how-to, in steps).
2. It names a method, not a person, brand, place or authority. It avoids 零一, which resembles a real AI company (portfolio.md 2A), and carries no 官方, 專家 or 老師 wording.
3. It is neutral in gender, age and region, so the design can stay neutral too.
4. It fits the studio's position: 明說 means saying things plainly, and steps are the plainest way to explain.
5. It supports the series device 「第 0 步」 (section 2.8): step zero is "check the date and source".

**Collision and risk flags** (nothing was searched; no clash can be claimed either way)

| Risk | Detail | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Very common word | Weak as a trademark and hard to own as a handle. Many how-to pages, channels and apps are likely to use 步驟 or "Step". **Unverified: none were checked** | TIPO search in Chinese and romanised forms (class numbers for the lawyer; legal.md 8.1); handle and business-name searches; consider a distinguishing suffix |
| Search noise | 步驟 appears in countless tutorial titles, so the channel name will not stand out in YouTube search results | Make the thumbnail tell (section 4.6) and the series tag 「第 0 步」 carry recognition |
| Pronunciation | The standard reading of 驟 may differ between Taiwan and the mainland (zòu versus zhòu). **Unverified recall**; it changes which romanised handle sounds right | Wife says how she and her friends pronounce it (open item 2) |
| Homophone with 不周 | The placeholder slug `buzhou` reads as 不周 ("not thorough"; also 不周山 in myth). That is an unfortunate meaning for a careful-steps character | Do not use `buzhou` as the public handle unless the wife says it is fine |
| Security phrase | 兩步驟驗證 (two-step verification) is a common security phrase, so the name may pull unrelated search traffic | Accept; it is topically close to pillar P4 |
| Implied authority | A name about "the steps" could suggest official or authoritative instructions | Every video says it is not affiliated with the tool vendor and that the official page prevails (section 8) |

**Fallback name if the reading test fails (idea only):** 第零步, which echoes the running joke and is more distinctive for trademark and handle purposes, but may sound odd in Mandarin; the wife decides. The portfolio does not list an alternate, so this is my suggestion.

### 2.5 Personality

Calm, methodical, patient. Dry rather than bubbly. Quietly self-aware about being an AI: it treats its own limits as the brand. It prefers a small, dated, checkable fact to a big claim. It admits "I cannot judge that" quickly and points to the official page. It never mocks a viewer's question, never cheerleads a tool, and never scares people with "AI will replace you" lines.

**Tone reference:** a tidy handout, not a hype video. (A style comparison, not a claim about anyone's experience.)

### 2.6 Values

1. **Date first.** Every fact has a date and a source (BRIEF ground rule 10).
2. **One step at a time.** Three steps at most per video; longer topics are split.
3. **Say what I cannot do.** I did not use, test or own anything; the studio's human did the recording.
4. **Official page prevails.** Tools change; I point to the primary source.
5. **Your data stays yours.** I never ask you to paste or send personal data.
6. **No pressure.** No urgency, no fear, no "must-have", no income promises.

### 3.2 How 步驟 refers to itself, and how it says "I" without claiming experience

- **Self-reference:** 「我」 in speech and replies; 「步驟」 only in the bio, intros and the first-line disclosure. Never 「小編」, 「本人」, 「人家」. Humans at the studio are 「工作室真人」 or 「工作室」 (never "we" for the character plus humans together, to keep the line between them clear).
- **Pronouns:** studio copy refers to the character by name or as 「這個角色」, not 他 or 她. Gender is not stated (section 4.1).
- **The honest division of labour:** the human operates; the character explains. Lines say so.

| Allowed "I" verbs (explain, organise, compare, point) | Not allowed (claims doing, using, testing or knowing from use) |
|---|---|
| 我來說明、我整理、我比對、我列出、我標出來源、我提醒、我補充、我更正、帶你看這段示範、我只整理公開資料 | 我用了、我裝了、我訂閱了、我跑了一下、我試著、我試過、我測過、我發現（用後得出的結論）、我常用、我習慣、我的設定、我的流程、我每天、我推薦 |

- **示範 (demonstration):** allowed as a noun or as 「帶你看示範」 (I take you through the demo). **Not** 「我來示範給你看」 when the screen shows an operation, because that reads as the character operating the tool. The wife may find a more natural phrasing (open item 6).
- **Attribution of vendor claims:** 「依官方頁面」「官方說明寫到」「廠商宣稱」 plus a date. Never present a vendor's marketing claim as the character's finding.
- **Negations are allowed in identity lines.** 「我沒有親手用過任何工具」 states non-experience (culture.md 2.4). A keyword scan will flag these; the wife confirms them as intentional. They appear only in disclosures, intros and replies about being AI.

### 5.7 Procedure to keep the same face every time

1. **Create the anchor.** Generate many candidates from 5.1 (record every prompt). A short list is chosen blind by the operator and the wife. One master is chosen.
2. **Finish by hand.** The operator traces or cleans the master in a vector editor, fixes proportions and the patch, and exports a transparent PNG and the vector file. This is the **identity source**, independent of any tool's drift, and also the human-authored layer (legal.md 8.2).
3. **Name files** `{slug}_{type}_{vNN}_{view}_{expr}_{outfit}_{YYYYMMDD}.png`, for example `buzhou_ref_v01_front_E01_A_20261015.png`. Rename with the real handle after the availability check. Keep old versions; never overwrite a master. (The `assets/{slug}/` layout is my proposal for Phase 4 to confirm.)
4. **Reference-image workflow.** For every new image attach the master and the closest-angle approved image (at most two or three references). The prompt starts with "same character as attached; do not redesign" (5.2, 5.3). Change one thing at a time.
5. **No telephone game.** If a result drifts, restart from the master, never from the previous generated image.
6. **Tick 5.6** for every image. Overlay the new image on the master (onion-skin) to see drift in the jaw, eyes and patch. Reject anything with a locked-feature fail.
7. **Seeds.** Where a tool exposes a seed value, record it in the provenance row. The consumer interfaces of Gemini and GPT image generation probably do not (assumption), so rely on reference images, not seeds.
8. **No real-person references.** Reference images are only our own approved masters, or abstract colour swatches. No photos of real people, no celebrity or creator names, no existing characters (BRIEF ground rule 2).
9. **Design changes** go through a bible revision and the wife's re-approval, followed by a fresh look-alike check.

**Look-alike check procedure (run on the master and again on any redesign; log result in `personas/{slug}/lookalike_log.md`, created in Phase 4)**

1. **Reverse-image search** the master and three variants with several public tools (for example Google Lens, Bing Visual Search, TinEye), by hand and logged out of personal accounts. Record the date, tool and top matches. No scraping behind logins.
2. **Blind panel.** Show the master to the operator, the wife and two or three trusted people (no engagement is being sought; this is not promotion). Ask: "Does this remind you of any real person, celebrity, VTuber, mascot or cartoon character?" Write down every answer.
3. **Named-comparison list.** Compare side by side with the Taiwan characters named in the research files (OPEN將, 虎妮, 光音凱伶, 宮祈緣, any competitor named in competitors.md), with common assistant and robot mascots, with well-known headphone-wearing virtual singers and VTubers, LINE Friends, Doraemon and Sanrio characters, brand mascots, with the other nine concepts in portfolio.md 2D, and with any logo that has stair or pixel motifs.
4. **Pass rule:** no match reported by search or panel, and no named-comparison similarity. Any match: redesign the feature or document why it is coincidence and ask the lawyer (legal.md question 20).
5. **Re-run** before launch, at the week-6 review, and after any complaint. A credible look-alike claim means take down within 24 hours and pause (portfolio.md 6.2).

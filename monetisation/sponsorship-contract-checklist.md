# Sponsorship and B2B contract checklist (Phase 8), DRAFT v0.1

Written 2026-10-12 for the operator (English, with the zh-TW term beside each Taiwan-specific concept), for the wife to check the zh-TW, and for a Taiwan lawyer (律師) to review. Studio name 明說角色工作室 / Plainspoken Studio is provisional and uncleared. Pilots: 方寸 (home organisation), 步驟 (AI tools), 色環 (outfit logic and colour).

**This is a checklist, not a contract, not a template and not legal advice. Nothing here has been sent, offered or signed, no client exists, and the business is not registered.** Every Taiwan-law point is **unverified, lawyer question**. Every zh-TW string is an AI draft for the wife. The contract itself is drafted or reviewed by the lawyer (`research/legal.md` section 12, Q24). Outreach and signatures are the humans' only.

Companion files: [studio-offer.md](studio-offer.md) (what is being sold) · [decline-list.md](decline-list.md) (what is refused) · [rate-card.md](rate-card.md) (proposed prices and the standing terms in its section 2) · [media-kits/](media-kits/) · [README.md](README.md) (the gates for the whole folder).

## 0. Read this first

1. **What it is for.** For each clause a sponsorship or B2B contract must cover: the studio's position, where the position comes from, how sure it is, and what a Taiwan lawyer must confirm (the last-but-one column). Use it three ways: to screen a brand's own draft (section 4), to brief the lawyer who writes the template (section 7), and as the last check before anyone signs (section 5).
2. **Where the positions come from.** The pilot bibles section 9.4 (what the contract must say), `rate-card.md` section 2 (standing terms) and section 3 (offer sheets), `media-kits/studio.md` section 9.1, `strategy/portfolio.md` 1.4 and 6, and `research/legal.md`. Nothing is invented: a default the studio chose is marked **assumption**.
3. **Four kinds of position** (read the Studio position column with these in mind):
   * **GR** is a Ground rule from `BRIEF.md` section 3. It is not negotiated. If a client will not accept it there is no deal.
   * **Policy** is a studio rule from a bible or the rate card (for example no exclusivity). It can move only by a written decision of the user and the wife, after the lawyer has been asked.
   * **Default** is the studio's opening position and is labelled **assumption** (for example a 12-month licence). It can be negotiated within the limits stated.
   * **Open** is a `[TO FILL WHEN REAL]` field. It is not filled with an estimate.
4. **Labels** in the Basis column: BIB = persona bible section 9.4 or 9.3; RC = `rate-card.md`; MK = `media-kits/`; PF = `strategy/portfolio.md`; LG = `research/legal.md`; GR n = Ground rule n; HT n = `HUMAN_TODO.md` item n.
5. **Lawyer to confirm** column: **Yes: Qn** points to a question already in `research/legal.md` section 12; **Yes: NEW-n** points to a question proposed in section 7 (not yet in `research/legal.md`); **記帳士** means the bookkeeper or accountant; **No** means a business decision for the user and the wife, or a Ground rule.
6. **No signature before** every item in section 5 is ticked. Today none is, and the studio cannot sign anything: it is not registered and the registrant is not decided (HT 4, 7).
7. **Real names stay out of the repo.** A filled copy for a real deal lives outside the repo or in the git-ignored `monetisation/media-kits/filled/`. This file never holds a client name, a contact name, a registration number, a bank detail or a price.

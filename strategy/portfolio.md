## 1. Studio

### 1.1 Chosen name (idea only; pending TIPO search and the wife's reading test)

| | zh-TW | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Recommended** | **明說角色工作室** (short form: 明說) | **Plainspoken Studio** |

Why: 「明說」 means to say something plainly, so the name itself states the positioning (openly virtual). 「角色」 tells readers these are characters. It uses everyday words only and names no person, place, brand or authority. It carries no 公司, 集團, 協會, 官方 or 銀行 wording.

Cautions: common words, so weak as a trademark and possibly already used. A TIPO trademark search (Chinese and romanised forms) plus handle and business-name checks are required before it is used anywhere. **Availability is not claimed.** 工作室 is a display label, not a legal entity form; the registered entity type (and whether the wife is registrant) is a 記帳士 and lawyer decision, and 公司 or 有限公司 must not appear in the public name unless true. The wife should say whether 明說 reads as warm and natural or stiff and legalistic (「我就明說了」 can also introduce blunt or bad news, so tone is a real question). The search should also cover look-alike and sound-alike forms (for example 名說, 明碩 and phonetic or romanised variants, and "Plainspoken" in English) in the goods and services classes the lawyer picks (likely advertising, entertainment, design and digital content; **class numbers not checked**), the 經濟部 business-name register, domains and the main platform handles. "Plainspoken" and 「明說」 may be read as a promise of candour; the studio must be able to back that, which is why the disclosure patterns in 1.4 are binding. No search of any register has been run, so no clash with a Taiwanese company or brand has been checked either way; this is not clearance.

### 3.3 Ground rule 8: the studio is named in every bio, but personas never vouch for each other

The bio line "created and run by Plainspoken Studio" is a **disclosure of common ownership**, not an endorsement. The rules that keep it that way (all binding, and written into the weekly operating routine):

1. Personas **never follow, like, comment on, tag, quote, duet or reply to each other**, and never share captions or scripts.
2. No persona recommends another persona or its content. If a viewer asks "who else do you know?", the reply is only that the studio makes other characters, with no praise and no link.
3. Cross-promotion, if any, is a **studio-level announcement** (one studio link page listing the characters neutrally, as a directory with no recommendation language), never persona to persona.
4. **No two personas promote the same brand or merchant in the same week** (a brand ledger by ISO week). Never pitch the same brand with FAS1 and a later FAS2.
5. A B2B client can never buy independent-sounding praise from a persona: no testimonial from one persona about another persona's work, and no persona vouches for a client it was not paid by.
6. Distinct voice, silhouette, palette and posting times per persona; no coordinated launches on the same day.
7. Studio affiliate links, gifted goods and boosted posts are disclosed on each persona's posts as in 1.4.
8. **No seeded popularity (Ground rule 7).** The operator's and wife's personal accounts, friends and relatives do not like, comment on, share or follow persona posts to lift them, and there are no engagement pods, follow-for-follow or like-for-like groups. ## 4. Pilot rationale

### 4.1 Why these three

The pilots are the user's fixed choice and the top three of 23 passing niches (3.85, 3.80, 3.70; scores within 0.25 are ties, so treat them as a set). They also work well as a set under all three lenses:

- **Risk (priority 1).** Lowest Ground-rule-6 contact in the research (R = 1, 2, 2); none is in the blocked or narrow-only tiers. - **Value without testimony.** Diagrams and arithmetic (N9), operator-run dated recordings (N7), colour and proportion rules plus brand-supplied photos (FAS1). Ground rule 4 is structural, not a matter of writer discipline.
- **Audience and engagement (priority 2).** Three different cells in age, platform and intent, so differences in results can be attributed to niche by platform, not to one shared audience. Rank stability: across five weightings N9 never falls below 7th (tied), N7 below 5th and FAS1 below 4th.
- **Platform standing.** N9 and N7 each have a non-Meta presence (YouTube), so one Meta action (the Instagram AI-generated-profile rule, announced 2026-08-31, Taiwan scope unconfirmed) cannot silence the whole pilot set. FAS1 is fully Meta (Instagram and Threads), the known exposure.
- **Revenue (priority 3).** Three different B2B buyer groups: fashion and accessory brands (FAS1, operator's warm contacts), home and storage e-commerce (N9), software and course vendors (N7). B2B creative needs no followers; follower income is upside.
- **Capacity.** 3 personas x 2 platforms x 3 posts = 18 approvals a week, inside Phase 1's workable 18 to 30, and (section 5) inside the operator's planning ceiling of 22. N9 needs diagrams, not faces; N7 shows a small presenter beside real recordings, so generated-face volume and image-QA exposure stay low.

### 6.1 Metric definitions and the threshold table

Window: weeks 3 to 6 after the persona's first post, median of **organic** posts (sponsored posts reported separately). Minimum sample: 8 organic posts per platform; if fewer (for example a pause), extend to week 8. Weeks 1 and 2 are warm-up; the rejection rate is scored on weeks 4 to 6. Metric availability by platform (reach, saves, link clicks) is **unverified** (HUMAN_TODO 10): record what each dashboard reports, apply thresholds per platform to the metrics available, and do not compare platforms with each other, with one exception: the platform-versus-platform tests H2 and H3 use a common-denominator metric, **(shares + comments) per 1,000 views**, because views is the one denominator all of Instagram, Threads and YouTube Shorts are expected to report (unverified). Saves are not expected on Threads or YouTube Shorts, so ER on those platforms is computed without saves and the "saves + shares" rows below are scored only where saves are reported. Minimum-sample warning: a wave-2 or later persona at 2 posts a week on one platform has exactly 8 organic posts in weeks 3 to 6, so any pause or skipped post triggers the extend-to-week-8 rule.

- **Reach:** unique accounts that saw the post (Instagram, Facebook); where a platform reports only views (Threads, YouTube Shorts), use unique viewers if offered, else views, and record which.
- **Engagement rate (ER) = (saves + shares + comments + link clicks) / reach**, per post, using only the terms the platform reports per post (link clicks are normally not reported per post, so in practice ER = (saves + shares + comments) / reach, with link clicks tracked separately below); persona figure = median across the window. Likes are excluded and reported separately.
- **Saves + shares per post:** median count, and their share of all counted interactions.
- **Link clicks:** clicks on the persona's tracked link-in-bio page (Phase 6, static HTML), counted per week at account level (they cannot be tied to a single post) and reported per 1,000 reach summed over the same week. A click counter (for example a privacy-respecting analytics tool or per-platform link parameters) is **not yet chosen**; it must be added in Phase 6 or 7 at no more than the NT$300 link-page line in section 7, and until then this row is unscored.
- **Review-rejection rate:** drafts the wife returns or rejects on first pass, as a share of drafts she reviews (any returned-with-changes counts).
- **Effort:** operator minutes per approval, all-in (pre-check, approval, rework; N7 recording tracked separately); wife minutes per master.
- **Cost:** studio-level tool spend (shared; never allocated to one persona), plus the minutes above.
- **Revenue:** NT$ received tagged to the persona for tracking only, and lead signals (written enquiry, accepted free sample, written quote, agency reply stating a budget).

### 6.4 What "scale" means, given capacity

Scale is **one step at a time, never two at once**: (a) add one post a week on a platform (maximum four), or (b) add the second platform at 2 posts a week, or (c) release a queued persona from the next wave early. It is allowed only if the capacity check passes: operator planned load at most 324 min and wife at most 510 min, both averaged over the last four weeks; total approvals at most 22 (Phase 1's upper figure of 30 is superseded: 30 approvals x 5 min plus 150 fixed and 60 N7 minutes is 360, leaving no rework reserve); studio spend within NT$5,000 (NT$10,000 only if a tool clearly earns it). If the check fails, scaling one persona must be paid for by a cut from 5.6. Scale never means more live personas than the ladder in 5.4 allows.

**Studio wave gate (to launch the next wave):** at least 2 of the 3 pilots are at Keep or better (for waves 3 and 4, at least 2 of the live personas in the previous wave); no safety incident open; registration complete and the reviewer trial passed; the capacity check above; wave-specific gates in 2.5 (Instagram AI-label check, lawyer answers before slots 9 and 10, written topic whitelist before slot 6, N7 distinct-audience check before slot 8).
